## 1. 네 번의 실패가 공유한 메커니즘 (자체 기록 분석)

- Every rejected topic tried to sell a MECHANISM or a PHENOMENON, while the lab's actual asset is a WORKLOAD and a MEASUREMENT DISCIPLINE. AC-HNSW proposed density-routed edge allocation and budgeted partial indexes; Propagated-RAG/DV-Pruner proposed MI-scored evidence pruning and a resolution/modality budgeter; CORAL proposed a natural-correlation benchmark axis; IntentStore proposed service-intent-driven storage. In each case the mechanism half was already published (SIEVE VLDB'25 for budgeted predicate sub-indexes, NaviX VLDB'25 and RACORN-1 for runtime predicate-density adaptation, CubeGraph and iRangeGraph for per-predicate partial graphs, RagVL/AKS/E-VRAG/Q-Frame/CARVE for query-aware evidence pruning and per-frame budgeting, ACL'26 utility-oriented visual evidence selection for information-gain pruning), and the phenomenon half was already published too (RACORN-1 for cluster-correlated recall collapse, SSMNBench ECCV'26 for 'extra simultaneous views hurt', SportMV-Agent for view selection, 'ANN Search: Recall What Matters' for answer scores staying stable under large recall drops). The unique thing the lab owned in each case — the non-circular tri-source workload, the paired real-vs-random controls, the pre-registered gates — was demoted to a testbed detail in the pitch and therefore carried none of the novelty.
- The lab's own robustness arm dissolves the effect its topic is built on. RQ5's headline (up to 0.627 recall loss under real clustered predicates) shrinks to +0.0098 on Weaviate sweeping, +0.0028 on Milvus graph, ≤+0.0014 on brute-force fallback and actually reverses sign (−0.0181) on Weaviate ACORN — one fortieth of the naive-FAISS effect. Design C's premise (index approximation reaching answers) is contradicted by the lab's own prefilter−vector +1.5pp p=0.448 null and by the mini-pilot's −0.020 leverage. So the phenomenon is real only in the naive execution regime nobody deploys, and any reviewer with a 2025-26 engine refutes the motivation. The lab keeps discovering this AFTER building a topic on the naive-path number rather than treating the naive-vs-robust gap as the finding itself.
- The load-bearing statistic was misnamed or internally inconsistent, so the topic was built on a number that did not mean what the proposal said. CORAL's 'natural correlation ρ = 0.616–0.895' is actually a Spearman rank correlation between per-predicate recall deficit and the global rank depth of exact filtered neighbors — not a predicate-embedding correlation at all; and the GLS sign convention makes the narrative backwards (positive local enrichment helps recall; it is negative GLS/local depletion that hurts). Propagated-RAG's '≥95% guaranteed answer propagation' is arithmetically impossible against the lab's own oracle ceiling of 75% (67/75 ≈ 89% already), and its 'mutual information between saliency and query terms' is undefined for a single frame–query pair. Definitional drift between an internal measurement and an external claim is the recurring proximate cause.
- Every topic that requires an EFFECT to exist has died at a pre-registered gate; the results that survive are nulls, boundaries and instrument failures. G1 (hardness-matched synthetic reproduces natural workload difficulty: 6/6 below SESOI, identical method rankings), P9/KG (no third signal exists by construction: lift median 0.002, KG-in-filter 0.176 < metadata-only 0.218), E-1 (index perturbation does not move delivered evidence: 27 configs all rel≈1.0), P0-C/P0-D (caption dominates every service intent; multiview rescue inconclusive), F5 (both candidate judges fail sensitivity/specificity with arm-differential misclassification 0.14–0.17). Five separate stopping rules, five stops. The gates are correctly placed at the load-bearing assumption, which is a strength — but it means the lab keeps selecting topics whose thesis is 'X helps' when the only claims its instruments can defend are 'X does not reach the outcome', 'the effect is regime-bound', and 'the measuring device is broken'.
- The unique data cannot be released, which removes the benchmark/resource route that the surviving gaps require. AI Hub 522, sinnaedoro, multi-angle and intelligent-CCTV corpora are Korea-gated (PIPA Art. 25; raw public CCTV needs a regulatory-sandbox data-trust zone), and even derived embeddings are uncertain. PVLDB EA&B and SIGIR/CIKM resource tracks expect public artifacts, so the red-team verdict on Design A was that MIRIS-plus-released-embeddings must carry the headline alone, with the good corpora relegated to replication. Any topic whose value proposition is 'we have data nobody else has' therefore converts into 'we have data nobody else can check'.
- Scale is fatal whenever the framing shifts from measurement to resource. 250 events, 3,000 clips, 85 queries, 776 queries and 26 hard queries are ample for a controlled measurement study but indefensible against SurveillanceVQA-589K (589K QA), TUMTraffic-VideoQA (85K QA), WTS/AI City Track 2 (annual community track with dozens of teams) or ForeSeaQA. The red-team repeatedly reached the same conclusion — defensible as an IR test collection or a measurement study, dead as a dataset paper — yet each new proposal reintroduced a resource/benchmark headline. Combined with a 6–12 month closing window on every surviving gap (five filtered-ANN benchmark papers in 2025-26; SIEVE §7.7 and HoneyBee §5.3 explicitly pre-announcing the online-lifecycle follow-up; ForeSea v2 likely to add a retrieval track), slow topic selection converts a real gap into a scooped one.

## 4. 종합 인벤토리 판정

This lab's genuinely scarce possession is not an algorithm or a dataset but a *validity apparatus*: a tri-source non-circular CCTV retrieval workload (3,000 clips / 85 queries / 6,809 strict + 24,872 semantic qrels) in which filter predicates come from one physical sensor camera, relevance from human annotators on other cameras, and searchable documents from pixel-only VLM captions, all enforced by a machine audit (A6 6/6) and scored twice under strict and semantic ground truth — an artifact an exhaustive international sweep concluded has no second instance. Around it sit instruments no competitor has assembled: a controlled circularity-injection experiment that prices a single circular edge at +0.63 to +0.83 nDCG and replicates it in two encoder spaces; paired real-predicate vs same-selectivity-random filtered-ANN measurements on two real 100K+ vector corpora showing naive paths overestimate recall by up to +0.627 while modern robust engine paths shrink that to +0.003–0.010 and Weaviate ACORN reverses sign; a pre-registered hardness-matching experiment (G1) that externally validated HCBGen's unvalidated core assumption; a 498-config propagation-tuple grid over four production vector DBs with character-span gold evidence and both closed-book (2.343) and oracle (3.823) anchors; a completed 80-case human audit yielding 79/80 faithful-but-incorrect answers with the decoy mechanism isolated on an independent anchor; and a five-judge measurement model showing correctness is a construct (ω .91–.99, inter-judge r .969) while LLM-judged faithfulness is not (ω .403, Prometheus Heywood −.91, inter-judge r −.127). The strongest unexploited material is all of the same kind — evidence that the measuring devices, not the systems, are what is broken: a judge that assigns Faithfulness 1 and 5 to the identical abstention string in 94.2% of repeated queries and gives 92.1% Faith=5 to a config that retrieved nothing at all; Correctness that is 96.4% explained by answer/abstain composition and ranks abstention below wrong answers; only 26.16% of measured rows ever activating the propagation path; a falsified D-optimality claim whose middle-layer η² values are 100% leakage; 58 of 70 evidence failures traced to embedding rank depth rather than ANN search; and 7–9B judges failing with opposite-direction biases and arm-differential misclassification that would corrupt the very comparison they are asked to score. Four rejected topics — AC-HNSW, Propagated-RAG/DV-Pruner, CORAL, IntentStore — failed by one mechanism repeated four times: each pitched a mechanism or a phenomenon (both of which 2024-26 groups already own and can produce faster), used the lab's unique validity apparatus merely as a testbed, and rested on a number that either dissolves under robust execution or was misnamed at the definition level (CORAL's ρ was a rank correlation between recall deficit and rank depth, not a predicate-embedding correlation; DV-Pruner's ≥95% propagation guarantee is arithmetically impossible against the lab's own 75% oracle ceiling). Two structural constraints compound this: the best corpora are legally non-releasable under PIPA, which kills the resource/benchmark route that EA&B and SIGIR resource tracks demand, and every corpus is small enough to lose a scale argument the instant the framing shifts from measurement to dataset. The defensible claim this lab can carry — and the only one its five self-fired stopping rules have never contradicted — is a claim about *where measurement breaks in retrieval-augmented pipelines*: that evaluation circularity, judge instrumentation, workload construction, and floor regimes each move reported quality by more than any system choice under study, quantified with paired and cluster bootstraps, negative controls, and pre-registered gates on real data in two independent domains. It should stop looking for an effect to own and start owning the boundary conditions and instrument failures it can already prove, publishing them as measurement contributions rather than as system or benchmark contributions.

## 5. 방향별 공백 탐색 결과

### 방향 1: 측정 도구 공백
**판정**: Yes, something is genuinely open here, but far less than Direction 1 assumes, and the specific sub-question the brief is most enthusiastic about — (c), validating LLM judges — is now one of the most crowded areas in the field and should be dropped as a standalone. 'Reliability without Validity' (arXiv 2606.19544) already ran 21 judges over ~541,000 judgments with test-retest and kappa-deflation analysis; JuStRank (2412.09569), 'Bias in the Loop' (2604.16790) and 'The Coin Flip Judge' (2606.13685) already establish that judge bias flips which system wins; and the statistical repair kit is commoditized, with at least six 2026 prediction-powered-inference papers explicitly aimed at debiasing LLM-judge evaluation (PRECISE 2606.05308, GLIDE 2605.31278, CollabEval 2607.05046, VC-PPI 2605.09918, 2604.03257, 2602.02229). On the IR side the relevance-assessor question is equally staked out (Dietz et al. 'LLM-Evaluation Tropes' 2504.19076, overrating 2602.17170, Gienapp et al. 2510.04633), so 'nobody has psychometrically validated judges in IR/DB venues' is simply not true any more — and the lab's own in-flight ECIR judge paper occupies whatever slice remained. Sub-question (a) is also weaker than it looks: eRAG (2404.13781) and 'ANN Search: Recall What Matters' (2606.04522) have both already published 'recall@k is a poor proxy' and both proposed replacements, so the only survivable version is the second-order one — reliability and attenuation correction, which neither performed and which no RAG paper I could find has ever applied. Sub-question (d) is not viable alone: DB venues have no meta-research track, any lab can audit reporting practice without owning a single asset, and it only becomes a paper when it rides on an empirically measured noise floor that the lab produced itself. Sub-question (e) is crowded with abstention benchmarks and abstention methods, but the metric-artifact inversion — that judged quality is a composition instrument and that faithfulness rubrics are maximized by retrieving nothing — is genuinely unoccupied and is the single most publishable thing sitting unexploited on the lab's disk. The one direction that is both open and structurally healthy is (b): the entire vector-DB benchmarking literature descends from ANN-Benchmarks' convention of publishing point estimates with no error bars, the only variance evidence in existence is single-factor and incidental (insertion order 12pp, 2405.17813), the exact generalizability-theory machinery has just been demonstrated on a different object (2607.13304), and — decisively for this lab — it can be executed entirely on public corpora and open engine containers, which is the only route that escapes the PIPA non-releasability trap that killed the resource framing four times. Two warnings. Three of the four candidates below re-use the lab's SEM/judge apparatus and will collide with its own ECIR paper unless the composition/attenuation/aliasing angle, not reliability, carries the headline. And the ANN benchmark-methodology genre is being claimed right now (2606.14193 in June 2026, 2607.00728 in July 2026, both pre-registered, both methodology-first), so candidate 2 has perhaps six to nine months before the obvious next paper appears — this lab's recurring failure mode is deliberating a real gap into a scooped one.

- **Composition, Not Quality: LLM-Judged RAG Scores Measure Whether the System Answered, Not How Well**
  - 주장: In the operating regime where most published RAG configuration studies actually sit, an LLM-judged quality score is a monotone function of the answer/abstain composition of the run, not of answer quality. Three deliverables: (1) a decomposition estimator that splits any reported factor effect (chunk size, top-k, engine, index knob) into a composition component (did the factor change the abstention rate?) and a conditional-quality component (did it change quality among answered rows?); (2) a pre-analysis diagnostic — the propagation activation rate, the fraction of rows where the manipulated factor can change the answer at all — with a declared minimum-activation gate below which a factor-effect table is not interpretable; (3) a demonstration that rubric-based faithfulness judges are MAXIMIZED by total retrieval failure, with a deterministic-contract control showing the judge assigns both the lowest and highest score to a byte-identical abstention string. The lab's own numbers already carry this: 96.7% of Correctness=1 mass is abstention; a composition-only model explains 96.4% of between-config Correctness variance; restricting to answered rows collapses chunk-size eta^2 from 0.74 to 0.05 and flattens Correctness to 3.55-3.61 across a 40-config grid; only 26.16% of 31,040 measured rows activate the propagation path; config-level corr(abstention rate, Faithfulness) = +0.979 with a zero-retrieval config scoring 92.1% Faith=5; 94.2% of repeated queries received both Faith=1 and Faith=5 for the same string.
  - 열린 이유: The abstention literature I could enumerate splits cleanly into (a) method papers proposing better abstention/selective-prediction and (b) benchmarks that ADD unanswerable items and report refusal rates — HopRefusalBench 2608.01358, MTRAG-UN 2602.23184, CRUMQs 2510.11956, RagRefuse 2510.10452, FaithEval 2410.03727. Neither family asks the inverse question: on ordinary answerable-only benchmarks, how much of a reported metric difference is composition rather than quality. No paper I found decomposes RAG metric variance into composition vs conditional quality, defines an activation-rate precondition for interpreting an ablation table, or shows that a faithfulness rubric rewards retrieving nothing. The abstention-as-confound observations that do exist are one-line caveats inside method papers, not an instrument analysis.
  - 최근접 선행: Sufficient Context, arXiv 2411.06037 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.06037) — stratifies RAG errors by whether context suffices, separates hallucination from correct abstention, and proposes a selective-generation fix worth 2-10%. It does NOT decompose reported metric variance into composition vs conditional quality, does NOT re-analyze published factor-effect / ablation tables, does NOT define an activation-rate precondition, and does NOT show that judged faithfulness increases as retrieval fails. Second nearest: 'Evaluating RAG Metrics in Applied Contexts', arXiv 2607.07302 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07302) — correlates Ragas / DeepEval / RAGChecker / Opik against two human raters on one business QA set; a correlational validation with acknowledged methodological limits, no composition model, no re-analysis of anyone's grid. Third: 'Over-Searching in Search-Augmented LLMs', arXiv 2601.05503 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.05503) — finds search helps answerable queries but harms abstention on unanswerable ones; treats this as a system behavior and a cost trade-off (TPC metric), not as a property of the measuring instrument.
  - 자산 적합성: Best fit of the four, and almost entirely already on disk. - **A Vector Database Benchmark Number Has No Error Bar: Variance Components and a Minimum Detectable Difference for ANN Retrieval Measurement**
  - 주장: The reported recall/latency of a vector-DB configuration is a random variable, and the field publishes it as a point estimate. Fit a crossed random-effects (generalizability-theory) model over the facets that a reader assumes away — build seed, insertion order, index-build thread count, engine container build, host state, and query sample — to produce (a) a variance-components table per engine and index family, (b) a dependability coefficient for a single measured number, and (c) a minimum detectable difference (MDD) at a stated design. Then ask the audit question: what fraction of the inter-method and inter-engine gaps reported in recent filtered-ANN and vector-DB papers falls inside that MDD. This turns 'use more trials' folklore into a costed reporting standard: how many builds x how many query samples buy you the right to claim a gap of size delta.
  - 열린 이유: ANN-Benchmarks (arXiv 1807.05614) established the reporting convention — recall/QPS Pareto curves, one build, no repeated trials, no confidence intervals — and every successor I enumerated inherits it, including the 2025-26 filtered-ANN benchmark wave (2509.07789, 2606.14193, 2602.11443). The only variance evidence I could find is single-factor and incidental: HNSW insertion order shifting recall by up to 12pp (2405.17813), a determinism claim used as a product feature (MonaVec 2606.19458, 'byte-identical within a build — a determinism guarantee that parallel-build graph libraries cannot offer'), and an 80%-of-queries non-determinism figure reported in passing by a context-governance paper (ContextNest 2607.02116, mean Jaccard 0.611). - **Is 'Retrieval Quality' One Construct? Reliability and Attenuation-Corrected Coupling Between Retrieval Metrics and Answer Utility**
  - 주장: Papers use recall@k, nDCG@k, context precision/recall, evidence-span coverage, eRAG, and 1/Ratio@k interchangeably as 'retrieval quality', and then correlate whichever one they picked against answer quality. Two claims: (1) these do not load on a single latent factor — a CFA/MTMM fit over configurations x queries separates at least a coverage factor from a rank-depth factor, with judge/method variance attached; (2) every one of them carries estimable unreliability at the query-sample level, so the retrieval->answer correlations the field reports are attenuated, and correcting for attenuation changes which retrieval decisions count as answer-visible. The concrete payoff is a table of reliability coefficients per retrieval metric and a disattenuated retrieval->answer coupling, which is the number a practitioner actually needs to decide whether tuning a retriever can ever show up downstream.
  - 열린 이유: Both prominent challenges to recall@k stop at correlation and then propose a replacement metric; neither estimates the reliability of the old metric or the new one, and neither treats 'retrieval quality' as a construct that could fail to be unidimensional. Generalizability theory for IR test collections exists (Urbano 2013/2016) but predates RAG and was never carried across the retrieval->generation boundary. Correction for attenuation — a century-old psychometric move — appears nowhere in the RAG evaluation literature I searched; the arXiv hits for attenuation + retrieval are optics, astronomy, and an unrelated GraphRAG reward term.
  - 최근접 선행: 'Evaluating Retrieval Quality in Retrieval-Augmented Generation' (eRAG), arXiv 2404.13781 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.13781) — establishes that traditional relevance labels have only small correlation with downstream RAG performance and proposes eRAG, which correlates better. It does NOT estimate the reliability of any retrieval metric, does NOT fit a measurement model, does NOT test unidimensionality, and does NOT disattenuate — its entire evidence base is raw correlation, which is precisely the quantity attenuation biases. 'ANN Search: Recall What Matters', arXiv 2606.04522 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04522) — shows label precision, semantic similarity, BERTScore and LLM-graded quality stay stable while Recall@k drops sharply, and proposes 1/Ratio@k; explicitly does no confidence-interval, measurement-error, or reliability analysis. Urbano et al., SIGIR 2013 (https://doi.org/10.1145/2484028.2484038) and Urbano, IRJ 2016 (https://doi.org/10.1007/s10791-015-9274-y) — generalizability-theory reliability for classic test collections; single-stage retrieval only, no generation stage, no cross-metric construct model.

- **Unidentified by Design: Factor Aliasing in RAG and Vector-Search Ablation Sweeps**
  - 주장: The dominant reporting device in RAG and vector-DB papers — a one-way effect table (eta^2, or delta-metric per factor) computed over a non-orthogonal configuration sweep — does not identify mid-sized factors, and the ranking it produces for everything below the top two is close to arbitrary. Deliverables: (1) a design diagnostic any paper can run on its own released config table (realized D-efficiency against random subsamples of equal size, plus the alias structure); (2) a stratified/marginal comparison showing how far marginal effects move once the design imbalance is absorbed; (3) a corrected estimator and a reporting rule stating which factors a given sweep can and cannot rank. The lab's own grid supplies the demonstration: realized D-efficiency 84.0% while all 200 random 498-row subsamples exceeded it (mean 98.2%); ef_construction 0.077 -> 0.003, chunk_overlap 0.031 -> 0.0002 (100% leakage), and chunk_method REVERSE-MASKED from 0.0027 -> 0.046 once stratified — a sign-level, not magnitude-level, failure. Only the top two factors and the floor were stable.
  - 열린 이유: I found no paper formalizing identifiability or design efficiency for RAG/vector-search ablation sweeps. The confound critiques that exist are all about ONE named nuisance variable in ONE setting — document length in factual-density RAG evaluation (2605.31506), pretraining-overlap in attribution (2605.26778), a five-arm protocol separating three specific effects in retrieval-warmed reasoning (2606.26476) — not about the structure of the design matrix that generates the effect table. Meanwhile the genre that would be corrected is expanding fast: component-ablation and design-search papers (RAISE 2605.30029, Dissecting Agentic RAG 2606.21553, 864-setting chunking studies 2605.04763) all publish exactly the kind of table this critique targets, and the community has started treating module-level GraphRAG ablation as a top-journal genre.

- **Middle-Rank Factors Are Noise: Analysis Validity of Fractional Configuration Grids in Vector-DB and RAG Ablations**
  - 주장: The dominant reporting style in vector-DB and RAG ablation papers — one-way eta^2 / marginal-mean factor-importance tables computed over a non-orthogonal fractional configuration grid — assigns middle-rank factor importance essentially at random; only the top factors and the floor are stable.
