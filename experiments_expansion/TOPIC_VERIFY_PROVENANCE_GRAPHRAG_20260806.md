### VERIFY-0 (DB 앵커)
Every DB-side anchor the document names exists and is venue-accurate (LOTUS = PVLDB 18(11) under the retitled 'Semantic Operators and Their Optimization: Towards AI-Based Data Analytics with Accuracy Guarantees'; TAG = CIDR 2025 with the <20% claim verbatim; TigerVector SIGMOD 2025 industrial; LEGO-GraphRAG VLDB 2025 E&A; SIC PVLDB 18(11):4073; CEDAR PVLDB 18(11):4492; Marchesin & Silvello PVLDB 17(9):2392 + PACMMOD 3(3) 2025), so the survey's factual layer holds. The document's real weakness is underweighting its two nearest neighbors, both in the same PVLDB issue: SIC already owns 'integrity constraints + grounding + auditable AI-augmented data systems' as vocabulary at pipeline level, and CEDAR already implements the exact claim-to-SQL-and-execute verification kernel that C1's 'every answer sentence back-traceable via SQL' proposes, just post-hoc over human-written summaries with cost-efficiency as the objective. Gap (1) therefore survives, but only in a narrowed form — generation-time provenance capture + integrity constraints + graph/vector/relational evidence for GraphRAG answers — and C1 is defensible only if SIC and CEDAR are explicit baselines it beats or subsumes. Gap (3) stands: Han et al. ### VERIFY-1 (NLP 앵커+구멍)
All seven NLP anchors verify, with two status corrections: AbstentionBench is now a NeurIPS 2025 paper (doc lists it as arXiv) and Sufficient Context is confirmed ICLR 2025, while Han et al. 2502.11371 remains arXiv-only with the doc's coverage numbers exactly right (65.8% HotpotQA / 65.5% NQ answer-entity coverage). HOLE 1 closes badly for C1's framing: sentence-level cite-while-generate is a mature, crowded line (ALCE EMNLP 2023, RARR ACL 2023, SelfCite ICML 2025, attributed QA still landing at ACL/EMNLP 2025 in legal and finance domains), SymGen already interleaves symbolic references into structured JSON fields, and 'Attribute or Abstain' (EMNLP 2024) already couples attribution with abstention — so C1 is only defensible as a DB-systems claim: deterministic why/how-provenance derived from query execution plus integrity-constraint checking across graph/vector/relational engines, i.e., guarantees where NLP offers learned, statistically-evaluated citations, and the paper must differentiate from this line explicitly or reviewers will kill it as 'attribution with extra steps'. The doc's gap (4) behind C3 is flatly stale: abstention grounded in structured evidence exists on both the SQL side (TrustSQL, PRACTIQ NAACL 2025, CIKM 2025 clinical detection, LatentRefusal at ACL 2026) and the KG side (RetinaQA ACL 2024 handles schema- and data-level unanswerability), and sufficiency-triggered selective abstention over retrieved context is published at ICLR 2025 (+2-10%).
