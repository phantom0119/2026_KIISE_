## 고위협 논문 원문 검증 결과 (7편)

### MLLM Is a Strong Reranker: Advancing Multimodal RAG via Knowledge-Enhanced Reranking and Noise-Injected Training (RagVL)
- 존재확인: True | arXiv preprint (arXiv:2407.21439, cs.AI/cs.CL/cs.LG); official repo cites it only as an arXiv preprint with no accepted venue (2024)
- C1(질의 인지 프루닝) 침범: True | C2(예산 문맥 최적화) 침범: False | RAG 미들웨어 위치: False
- 판정: RagVL directly occupies DV-Pruner's core territory: it instruction-tunes an MLLM to score each CLIP-retrieved image conditioned on the query (softmax probability of answering "Yes" to a relevance prompt), then threshold-filters (eta=0.5 or adaptive) to top-N before the generator sees anything — i.e., query-aware pruning of retrieved visual evidence as an explicit stage between retriever and VLM, and it even shares the proposal's motivating observation that generators fail on noisy/distractor retrieved images (noise-injected training at data and token levels addresses residual distractors). However, it is entirely image-based multimodal QA (WebQA, MultimodalQA), not video, not multi-view CCTV, and not framed as video-DBMS middleware; its pruning signal is a learned relevance probability, not the proposed mutual-information between visual saliency/attention and query terms. It does nothing in contribution-2 territory: top-K=20 and top-N are fixed per dataset, captions are always included, and there is no per-query Pareto allocation of resolution, frame count, or frame-vs-caption mix under context/GPU budgets (efficiency is only a post-hoc appendix note). Net: contribution 1 must be differentiated as video/multi-view-specific and training-free/saliency-based versus RagVL's tuned image reranker, while contribution 2 and the propagation-guarantee framing remain clear of this paper.
- 핵심 수치: Reranking lifts WebQA image retrieval R@2 to 82.00% (+24.90pp over CLIP) and MultimodalQA R@1 to 98.26% (+13.48pp); QA EM on MultimodalQA reaches 68.70% with InternVL2-1B (+18.83pp over CLIP Top-N injection); WebQA QA 64.25% with InternVL2-2B (+22.85pp over no-RAG) vs an oracle-evidence ceiling of 66.41%; reranker inference overhead ~5-6s noted but not optimized.

### Adaptive Keyframe Sampling for Long Video Understanding (AKS)
- 존재확인: True | CVPR 2025 (2025)
- C1(질의 인지 프루닝) 침범: True | C2(예산 문맥 최적화) 침범: False | RAG 미들웨어 위치: False
- 판정: AKS directly overlaps DV-Pruner's core mechanism: it performs training-free, query-conditioned selection of visual evidence (frame-prompt relevance scored by BLIP/CLIP, balanced against temporal coverage via a recursive judge-and-split optimization) before frames enter the MLLM, so "query-aware pruning of frames before VLM injection" is already published territory. The key difference is pipeline position: AKS samples from a single raw long video fed directly to an MLLM, whereas the proposal prunes Top-K multimodal evidence returned by a vector-DB retrieval layer in a video-DBMS/RAG setting, and DV-Pruner's mutual-information saliency scoring and distractor/negative-frame focus (yes-bias, position bias) go beyond AKS's relevance-plus-coverage objective. AKS does not touch contribution 2: its frame budget is fixed (16/32/64) with only which frames varying, and it never adapts resolution, frame count, or frames-vs-caption mix per query, nor optimizes GPU cost or claims any retrieval-to-answer propagation guarantee. Propagated-RAG must therefore cite AKS as strong prior art for query-aware frame selection and differentiate on the retrieval-middleware setting, distractor-robustness motivation, and the budgeted resolution/modality-mix optimization.
- 핵심 수치: LongVideoBench: +5.0pp (Qwen2-VL 55.5→60.5), +4.5pp (LLaVA-OV 54.8→59.3), +3.8pp (LLaVA-Video 58.9→62.7); VideoMME: +2.3pp / +1.9pp / +0.9pp for the same models; fixed frame budgets of 16/32/64 keyframes; training-free plug-and-play module.

### E-VRAG: Enhancing Long Video Understanding with Resource-Efficient Retrieval Augmented Generation
- 존재확인: True | arXiv preprint (arXiv:2508.01546; no peer-reviewed venue verified) (2025)
- C1(질의 인지 프루닝) 침범: True | C2(예산 문맥 최적화) 침범: False | RAG 미들웨어 위치: True
- 판정: Verified from the arXiv abs page and full-text HTML: E-VRAG is a training-free, query-conditioned frame-selection sandwich placed before the main VLM — hierarchical query decomposition into entity/knowledge/causal captions matched by CLIP for pre-filtering, then lightweight-VLM relevance scoring with a score-distribution-based retrieval rule — cutting ~70% compute while raising accuracy, which squarely overlaps DV-Pruner's core claim of query-aware evidence pruning between retrieval and generation. However, its "retrieval" is internal to a single long video (uniform sampling of 256 candidates → 64 frames), not Top-K evidence coming out of a multi-video vector-DB/DBMS layer with cross-camera distractors, and its scoring is CLIP-caption similarity plus small-VLM relevance rather than mutual information between visual saliency/attention and query terms. It contains no per-query budget allocation over frame resolution, frame count, or frame-vs-caption mix (frames are fed at a fixed dynamic-resolution setting and fixed counts), so the Task-Aware Context Budgeting contribution and any "answer propagation efficiency" guarantee are untouched. It also does not analyze the recall-to-answer propagation disconnect, yes-bias, or position bias; novelty for Propagated-RAG must therefore rest on the vector-DBMS middleware setting, the MI-based saliency scoring, the budgeting/Pareto layer, and the propagation-guarantee framing rather than on query-aware frame pruning per se, which E-VRAG already demonstrates.
- 핵심 수치: ~70% computational-cost reduction (single-view QA; ~50% vs online video-RAG baselines) while improving accuracy, training-free; Video-MME 65.4 (vs BOLT 64.6), MLVU 70.2 (vs 70.3), LongVideoBench 63.1 (vs 62.2), NextQA 84.0 (vs LLaVA-Video 83.8); pipeline uniformly samples 256 candidate frames and retrieves 64; lightweight ~2B VLM scores frames, LLaVA-Video-7B answers.

### ResAdapt: Adaptive Resolution for Efficient Multimodal Reasoning
- 존재확인: True | arXiv preprint (cs.CV/cs.AI/cs.CL, marked "work in progress"; no peer-reviewed venue) (2026)
- C1(질의 인지 프루닝) 침범: False | C2(예산 문맥 최적화) 침범: True | RAG 미들웨어 위치: False
- 판정: ResAdapt (arXiv 2603.28610, submitted 2026-03-30) is a real, verified preprint whose Allocator predicts per-frame visual resolution budget from coarse visual features plus the query (gated cross-attention to the query), trained as a contextual bandit with Cost-Aware Policy Optimization — so it already delivers the query-conditioned, cost-aware per-frame-resolution Pareto optimization that is roughly half of the proposal's Task-Aware Context Budgeting claim (contribution 2), and it does so with a learned real-time policy rather than a heuristic. However, it is strictly input-side within a single MLLM on uniformly sampled frames: there is no retrieval step, vector DB, or RAG anywhere; every frame keeps nonzero resolution (s_min > 0), so it never prunes/drops distractor frames, and it does not touch frame count per query or the frames-vs-captions modality mix. It therefore does not occupy DV-Pruner territory (contribution 1) nor the retrieval-to-generator middleware / answer-propagation framing. Net effect: the proposal's per-frame resolution budgeting is no longer novel in isolation and must be positioned as extending ResAdapt-style allocation into a retrieval-aware setting (distractor pruning, frame-vs-caption mix, propagation-rate guarantee), citing it as the closest budgeting baseline.
- 핵심 수치: Supports up to 16x more frames at the same visual budget with over 15% performance gain (VideoMMMU under aggressive compression, ~11% retention); improves low-budget operating points and lies on/near the efficiency-accuracy frontier across VideoMME, LongVideoBench, MMVU, MLVU, LVBench, Charades-STA, ActivityNet, NExT-GQA.

### Q-Frame: Query-aware Frame Selection and Multi-Resolution Adaptation for Video-LLMs
- 존재확인: True | ICCV 2025 (2025)
- C1(질의 인지 프루닝) 침범: True | C2(예산 문맥 최적화) 침범: True | RAG 미들웨어 위치: False
- 판정: Q-Frame overlaps substantially with both claimed contributions at the mechanism level: it selects frames conditioned on the query (CLIP text-image similarity + Gumbel-Max sampling) before VLM injection, and it allocates a hard token/compute budget across frame count and per-frame resolution (three resolution tiers under the constraint K + M/4 + N/16 = 8 equivalent frames), which is exactly DV-Pruner-style query-aware pruning plus resolution-level context budgeting. However, it is NOT retrieval-to-generator middleware: the abstract and method operate on a single input video uniformly pre-sampled to 128 candidate frames, with no vector DB, no retrieved Top-K evidence set, no distractor/negative-evidence problem, and no mention of RAG anywhere. Its budget allocation is also a fixed rank-stratified configuration (relevance rank determines resolution tier), not a real-time Pareto optimization, and it never trades frames against text captions nor claims any retrieval-to-answer propagation guarantee. Propagated-RAG must therefore differentiate on the RAG/DBMS middleware setting (multi-source retrieved evidence with distractors, propagation-rate metric, frame-vs-caption mix) rather than on the bare idea of query-aware frame selection plus multi-resolution budgeting, which Q-Frame already published at ICCV 2025.
- 핵심 수치: Qwen2-VL: MLVU 56.9->65.4 (+8.5pp), LongVideoBench 53.5->58.4 (+4.9pp), Video-MME w/o subs 56.2->61.0 (+4.8pp); on >15-min videos +10.5pp (Qwen2-VL) and +7.5pp (GPT-4o). Processes 44 effective frames (4 high + 8 medium + 32 low res) vs 8 uniform within ~3.5% extra tokens (~2,345 tokens/video), ~180ms selection overhead on 128 candidate frames. Plugs into VILA-1.5-8B, Qwen2-VL-7B, GPT-4o.

### Query-Conditioned Evidential Keyframe Sampling for MLLM-Based Long-Form Video Understanding
- 존재확인: True | arXiv preprint (cs.CV / cs.AI / cs.LG), no peer-reviewed venue listed (2026)
- C1(질의 인지 프루닝) 침범: True | C2(예산 문맥 최적화) 침범: False | RAG 미들웨어 위치: False
- 판정: This paper directly occupies the theoretical core of DV-Pruner's claimed novelty: it formulates query-conditioned frame selection as maximizing conditional mutual information between selected frames and the query (information-bottleneck grounding), decomposes it into per-frame evidential scoring, and trains a query-conditioned scorer with an InfoNCE contrastive objective — so "MI-based query-frame saliency scoring before VLM injection" cannot be claimed as new in itself. However, it operates strictly on a single long video with no retrieval component: frames come from uniform segmentation of one video, not from a vector-DB Top-K, and the total frame count m is fixed by the MLLM context window rather than optimized per query. It therefore does none of contribution 2 (no adaptive allocation across resolution, frame count, or frames-vs-captions mix, and no propagation-rate guarantee) and is not positioned as retrieval-to-generator middleware in a video RAG/DBMS pipeline; multi-video or RAG settings are not even mentioned as future work. Propagated-RAG must cite it and reposition DV-Pruner's novelty as extending MI-based evidential scoring to retrieved cross-video/multi-view evidence with distractor clips (the journal's recall-to-answer disconnect), plus the budgeting layer, rather than claiming the MI scoring objective per se.
- 핵심 수치: LVBench: +10.1pp accuracy (47.7% vs 37.6%) with Qwen2.5-VL-7B, beating TSPO and ReTaKe at identical frame budgets; Video-MME: +2.9pp overall (63.6% vs 60.7% uniform sampling), +4.4pp on the long-video subset; training cost 0.6h vs 78h for the RL baseline TSPO (~130x faster).

### Rethinking RAG in Long Videos: What to Retrieve and How to Use It? (CARVE + V-RAGBench)
- 존재확인: True | arXiv preprint (cs.AI), no publication venue listed (2026)
- C1(질의 인지 프루닝) 침범: True | C2(예산 문맥 최적화) 침범: False | RAG 미들웨어 위치: True
- 판정: CARVE is close prior art on both the motivation and the architecture slot: V-RAGBench explicitly decouples retrieval from generation evaluation to expose the retrieval-to-answer disconnect (the proposal's RQ6 finding), and CARVE is exactly a middleware layer between the retriever and the VLM that applies query-conditioned cross-encoder scoring to decide which retrieved chunks, in which modality (frames vs text captions) and temporal granularity, enter the generator — overlapping DV-Pruner's query-aware evidence selection and part of Contribution 2's frames-vs-captions mix decision. However, it re-ranks and re-formats whole chunks rather than pruning distractor frames within evidence via visual saliency/mutual information, and it does not target VLM yes-bias or position-bias explicitly. It performs no budget-constrained optimization: no token/GPU-cost limit, no per-frame resolution adaptation, no frame-count allocation, no Pareto frontier, and no propagation-efficiency guarantee — its latency win is a by-product of passing only winning configurations. Remaining differentiation for Propagated-RAG: intra-evidence saliency-based frame pruning, cost/context-budgeted joint optimization of resolution + modality mix, the >=95% propagation guarantee, and the multi-view CCTV/traffic domain (CARVE is egocentric long video), but the novelty claim of "chunk/modality selection middleware in video RAG" is already taken and must be cited and differentiated.
- 핵심 수치: V-RAGBench: 2,100 query-evidence-answer triplets over 216 egocentric videos (11-99h; Ego4D + EgoLife). CARVE beats 8 VideoRAG baselines: Recall@5 0.603 vs 0.510 (best baseline VideoRAG-A); pass rate 0.357 vs 0.315 with Qwen3-VL-8B; beats trained query-level routers (0.329-0.310) without training. Latency 3.4s/query passing only winning configurations vs 8.2s concatenation; full 4-config retrieval 4.6s.

## 각도별 결론

### [rag-context-pruning]
For this angle the claimed gap is at best half real. The core mechanism of contribution 1 — a query-conditioned score-and-prune middleware sandwiched between retrieval and generation — is fully occupied in text RAG (Provence, RECOMP, FILCO, LongLLMLingua, plus Selective-Context EMNLP 2023 which is query-agnostic, xRAG NeurIPS 2024 which needs a trained bridge, and prompting-based Astute RAG) and is already occupied in the multimodal/video domain: RagVL filters top-k retrieved images with an MLLM reranker plus noise-injected training, E-VRAG does training-free query-aware frame scoring and filtering inside a cost-constrained video-RAG pipeline, and AKS does query-relevance keyframe selection before a video MLLM, so DV-Pruner's residual novelty shrinks to its specific scoring signal (mutual information between visual saliency and query terms) and the multi-camera CCTV vector-DB deployment. The retrieval-to-answer disconnect motivating the paper is likewise well documented in text RAG (Sufficient Context ICLR 2025, The Power of Noise SIGIR 2024 with its position-bias and distractors-can-help findings), so the journal's RQ6 is a multimodal replication of a known phenomenon rather than a new one — and Power of Noise even complicates the premise that distractors always hurt. Contribution 2 retains a genuine open slice: nothing found jointly Pareto-optimizes per-frame resolution AND the frame-vs-caption modality mix in real time inside a video-RAG loop, but its two ingredients are separately taken (ResAdapt and CARES for query-aware resolution budgeting, LongLLMLingua for budget-controlled compression), and the ">=95% answer propagation efficiency guarantee" has no prior art mainly because it is an empirical promise dressed as a guarantee, which reviewers at SIGMOD/NeurIPS will attack. Recommended positioning: frame both contributions as the first integrated, cost-budgeted evidence-selection layer for multi-camera video DBMS RAG and cite RagVL/E-VRAG/AKS as baselines to beat, not as absent prior work.

### [frame-selection-videollm]
For contribution 1 the claimed gap is mostly not real: query-aware, training-free frame selection before VLM injection is the densest space in video-LLM research, with AKS, BOLT, Q-Frame, MDP3, VideoTree, QuoTA and LVNet all delivering plug-and-play query-conditioned pruning, and even the mutual-information framing is already taken (arXiv 2604.01002 maximizes conditional MI between frames and query via information bottleneck; arXiv 2601.07459 uses submodular MI). The narrower claim that nothing operates on RETRIEVED top-K evidence in a RAG pipeline is also eroding fast in 2026: CARVE reranks retrieved chunks and picks per-chunk frames-vs-text modality, the ACL 2026 MAGMaR pipeline explicitly prunes logically irrelevant distractors from retrieved candidates, FastV-RAG filters retrieved knowledge, and BOLT's multi-source retrieval setting already evaluates selection under distractor-video contamination. Q-Frame additionally couples selection with per-frame multi-resolution adaptation, cutting into contribution 2's resolution-budgeting claim as well. What genuinely remains open in this angle: MI computed specifically between visual attention/saliency maps and query terms (competitors use CLIP similarity, LVLM scoring, or embedding kernels, not attention-saliency MI), the multi-camera CCTV/surveillance evidence setting, and any quantified retrieval-to-answer propagation guarantee; DV-Pruner as currently stated would be judged incremental unless repositioned around those three residuals.

### [token-pruning-resolution-budget]
For contribution 2, every individual knob the proposal claims is already taken: static-budget token pruning is saturated (FastV, DivPrune, VisionZip, PyramidDrop, SparseVLM), per-query adaptive resolution selection exists at NeurIPS 2025 (VisionThink, plus ViCO/Q-Frame/LongVU for query-conditional video fidelity), real-time inference under a hard latency/cost budget exists at ICCV 2025 (AdaLLaVA), adaptive budget allocation across video segments and image partitions exists (AdaReTaKe, HiRED), and even the explicit "budget-aware Pareto frontier" framing is claimed by VisPCO (2026) and the inference-optimal scaling-law paper — so any pitch of "first real-time cost-quality-adaptive visual budgeting" would be refuted in review. What no found paper does is the specific joint optimization the proposal describes: allocating a shared context/GPU budget per query ACROSS multiple retrieved evidence items in a RAG pipeline while simultaneously choosing the frames-vs-text-captions mix ratio (Text-or-Pixels only measures the exchange rate; no work jointly allocates between modalities under retrieval). That residual gap is real but far narrower than claimed, and the ">=95% guaranteed answer propagation" framing is an empirical target, not a provable guarantee, and will not survive as a differentiator. The paper is defensible only if repositioned as RAG-level cross-evidence, cross-modality budget allocation explicitly contrasted against VisionThink, AdaLLaVA, AdaReTaKe, and HiRED as ablation baselines.

### [disconnect-position-bias]
For this angle the claimed gap is at best half-real: the retrieval-quality → answer-quality disconnect is now thoroughly documented prior art in both text RAG (eRAG SIGIR 2024; Sufficient Context ICLR 2025; Long-Context LLMs Meet RAG ICLR 2025) and multimodal/video settings (MRAG-Bench's oracle-utilization gap; Visual Haystacks' distractor susceptibility; Lost-at-the-End's primacy bias inside multimodal RAG lists; VideoHallucer's yes-bias metrics), so the RQ6 finding can only be framed as a domain-specific replication on multi-view CCTV, not a discovery. Worse for contribution 1, several papers already FIX the problem with system components in the same sandwich position: MIRAGE's query-aware image filter (ICLR 2025), AKS keyframe selection (CVPR 2025), and especially the ACL 2026 Utility-Oriented Visual Evidence Selection paper, which already does training-free information-gain (mutual-information-style) scoring of retrieved visual evidence — DV-Pruner must differentiate on the attention/saliency×query signal and the vector-DB-middleware setting or it is incremental. For contribution 2, the 2026 CARVE/V-RAGBench work already measures decoupled retrieval-vs-generation propagation in video RAG and adaptively picks modality-granularity (frames-vs-text) configurations per chunk, overlapping the frames-vs-captions mix half of the budgeter. What remains genuinely open: no found paper jointly Pareto-optimizes per-frame resolution × modality mix under an explicit GPU-cost budget in real time, none offers any guaranteed propagation-efficiency level (the ">=95%" claim is unclaimed but also likely unprovable), and the multi-view CCTV selection-beats-accumulation observation appears unpublished — those are the defensible slivers.

### [video-rag-systems]
On the system-framing angle the claimed gap is only partially real: 2024-2026 produced a dense field of end-to-end video-RAG systems (two VideoRAGs, Video-RAG, iRAG, Goldfish/ECCV 2024, DrVideo/CVPR 2025, VideoAgent/ECCV 2024, NVIDIA's production VSS blueprint, plus video-DBMS+LLM work at SIGMOD 2025 from the Balazinska group), and several already insert an evidence-filtering stage between retrieval and generation — Vgent's structured verification of retrieved clips, E-VRAG's training-free frame pre-filtering and scoring with ~70% compute savings, RagVL's VLM reranker with adaptive-threshold filtering, and even the KAIST VideoRAG baseline's informative-frame selection — while explicit context/token budgeting is emerging fast (AKS's fixed-token-budget keyframe optimization, VideoRouter's context-window-derived budget-constrained compression, AdaVideoRAG's query-complexity cost adaptation). The single closest threat to DV-Pruner is the 2026 utility-oriented evidence-selection paper, which already does training-free, information-gain-based pruning of retrieved multimodal evidence, albeit on images rather than multi-camera video. What remains genuinely open is the specific combination: a pruning+budgeting middleware sited inside a vector-DB/DBMS layer over a multi-camera CCTV corpus, joint real-time Pareto optimization of per-frame resolution together with the frames-vs-captions mix ratio, and any measured 'retrieval-to-answer propagation rate' with a >=95% guarantee — no surveyed system optimizes or guarantees propagation as a first-class metric (though a hard guarantee will be difficult to defend technically). The paper cannot claim to be the first video-RAG system with evidence pruning or token budgeting; it must position against Vgent, E-VRAG, AKS, and the utility-selection line and argue novelty via the DBMS placement, the joint resolution/modality-mix optimization, and the propagation metric itself.

### [latest-2025-2026]
In the mid-2025 to 2026 window, both claimed contributions are heavily encroached but not exactly delivered. For contribution 1, ACL 2026's Utility-Oriented Visual Evidence Selection already ships training-free, information-gain-based pruning of retrieved multimodal evidence as middleware (images, not video), MI-Pruner claims the crossmodal mutual-information scoring mechanism itself, and RagVL plus the coarse-to-fine video-RAG pipeline already put reranking/filtering stages between retrieval and the (V)LM — so 'MI/saliency-scored pruning of retrieved evidence' will read as incremental unless positioned as video-clip-level, multi-view-CCTV-specific distractor removal. For contribution 2, Q-Frame (ICCV 2025) already does query-aware per-frame multi-resolution allocation under compute limits and the Modality-Utility paper does cost-aware per-query text-vs-visual escalation, so the only genuinely open slice is the joint, per-query, real-time Pareto optimization over frame resolution AND frame-vs-caption mix inside a vector-DB-to-VLM pipeline — and the '>=95% answer propagation efficiency guarantee' has no precedent precisely because it is an empirical target, not a provable guarantee, which reviewers will challenge. The motivating disconnect finding is no longer novel in general form (MAD-RAG diagnoses retrieval hurting LVLM answers mechanistically, CFVBench shows the retrieval-to-generation bottleneck in video RAG, and a text-RAG study states the identical retrieval-does-not-guarantee-answers thesis), and ForeSea (ECCV 2026) already claims the first surveillance-forensics VideoRAG system, eroding the system-domain novelty too. What survives as defensible: the multi-view CCTV instantiation with viewpoint selection-beats-accumulation evidence, clip-level (not token-level) MI pruning of vector-DB-retrieved video evidence, and the joint resolution+modality-mix Pareto controller with propagation-rate as an explicit optimization target — provided the paper cites and differentiates against all of the above rather than claiming the gap is unoccupied.

## 완전성 비평 (critic) — 방법론 결함 지적 포함

Completeness verdict: the sweep missed an entire certified-guarantee lane — C-RAG (ICML 2024), TRAQ (NAACL 2024), RobustRAG (ICLR 2025), and above all RULE (EMNLP 2024), which already delivers provable risk-controlled selection of how many retrieved contexts a multimodal VLM sees — so the ">=95% guaranteed answer propagation" pitch is not just unprovable-as-stated, its rigorous version is partially occupied; it also skipped the video-DBMS systems lane a SIGMOD reviewer lives in (NoScope/BlazeIt lineage through Zelda, Seiden, and Balazinska's SIGMOD 2025 VOCAL-UDF) and industrial stacks beyond NVIDIA (Twelve Labs' Marengo+Pegasus running productionized vector-DB video RAG on Weaviate/Pinecone/Bedrock; VideoDB literally selling a "video database for AI" with scene-level RAG retrieval), plus query-aware allocation rows the prose named but never listed (LongVU ICML 2025, PruneVid ACL 2025 Findings) and the attention-as-MI formalism itself (QUITO-X EMNLP 2025 Findings). Methodological red flags a reviewer will raise: (a) the 95% figure is internally inconsistent with the journal's own data — oracle evidence yields only 75% accuracy, so conditional retrieval-to-answer conversion cannot hit 95% without redefining the metric, and if the metric is the retrieved/oracle ratio the journal already sits at 67/75≈89%, making "95%" a ~6pp empirical bump dressed as a guarantee that would need C-RAG/TRAQ-style conformal machinery to defend; (b) "mutual information between visual attention/saliency and query terms" is not well-defined for a single frame-query pair (no joint distribution to estimate) and will collapse in practice to attention-weighted similarity — already claimed by PruneVid, QUITO-X, and MI-Pruner — while obtaining attention/saliency itself costs a (surrogate-)VLM forward pass that must be charged against the claimed savings, as E-VRAG does; (c) the named baselines are 2024-era strawmen — the paper must instead beat E-VRAG, Vgent, AKS/BOLT/Q-Frame, RagVL, and a trivial CLIP-similarity rerank — and the evaluation (600 VQA + 250 events + Korea-gated AI Hub, no public benchmark such as V-RAGBench, CFVBench, ForeSeaQA, or SUTD-TrafficQA) is too small and too irreproducible to support any 95%-level claim (a 250-event proportion carries roughly ±5pp CI); (d) the journal's own numbers show irrelevant evidence HELPS (+22pp over no-evidence), echoing Power-of-Noise and undercutting the prune-the-distractors premise, and pruning does not address the cited position/yes biases at all, so cheap reordering ablations (LongLLMLingua reordering, SoFA) must be run before crediting the pruner. Remaining uncovered modalities: Korean-language literature around the AI Hub CCTV/intersection datasets (likely nearest-neighbor domestic prior work), patents, and the trained-robust-generator lane beyond RAFT (Self-RAG, Yoran et al. ICLR 2024, Chain-of-Note — well-known but not re-verifiable this session after the search budget capped). Errors in the current list: RagVL is double-counted as two rows with inconsistent threat labels ("MLLM Is a Strong Reranker", arXiv 2024, threat=high vs "VLM Is a Strong Reranker", EMNLP 2025 Findings, threat=medium — same system), and the "SIGIR 2026 'The Powerless Noise' follow-up" embedded in the Power-of-Noise row could not be verified and should be re-checked before it is cited anywhere.

## 내부 근거 대조 (60_REVIEW 감사 기준, 2026-08-04 세션 확인)

- ANN→답변 전파는 '증명된 단절'이 아니라 **판정 불능**: 소형=검색 조작 실패, 대형=360건 중 적중 32건, S4 게이트(처치 5%p) 미달로 호출 중단. 저널은 공헌을 '전파 조건 진단'으로 강등함.
- yes-bias는 이진 존재 질문에서 실측 확인(→3지선다 교체 이력) — 편향 서사의 실측 근거.
- 무관 문맥이 무증거 대비 +23.0%p로 오히려 도움(Power-of-Noise와 일치) — 'distractor 해악' 전제와 부분 충돌. 프루닝의 진짜 내부 근거는 선택>축적(단일 양시점 +15.2%p, 2시점 동시 무이득)과 oracle−vector +8.2%p 헤드룸.

### 논문별 한 줄 요약

- **MLLM Is a Strong Reranker: Advancing Multimodal RAG via Knowledge-Enhanced Reranking and Noise-Injected Training (RagVL)** (arXiv 2024): Instruction-tunes an MLLM as a reranker that scores and filters the top-k retrieved images against the query before generation, and noise-injects training to make the generator robust to residual distractor images (+40% retrieval accuracy, 41.6%->63.7% QA on InternVL2-1B); this IS query-aware multimodal evidence pruning between retriever and VLM. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21439]
- **Adaptive Keyframe Sampling for Long Video Understanding (AKS)** (CVPR 2025): Training-free, query-aware keyframe selection formulated as an optimization over prompt-frame relevance and temporal coverage (judge-and-split), choosing which frames enter the MLLM under a fixed frame budget; delivers query-conditioned visual evidence selection before VLM injection, though from raw video rather than from vector-DB retrieval results. [https://github.com/ncTimTang/AKS]
- **E-VRAG: Enhancing Long Video Understanding with Resource-Efficient Retrieval Augmented Generation** (arXiv 2025): A video-RAG pipeline that pre-filters frames via hierarchical query decomposition, scores frames with a lightweight VLM (using the score distribution to select), then answers with the main VLM, cutting ~70% compute while raising accuracy, training-free; a cost-aware query-conditioned frame-pruning sandwich for video RAG — the closest single overlap with DV-Pruner. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.01546]
- **ResAdapt: Adaptive Resolution for Efficient Multimodal Reasoning** (arXiv 2026): A lightweight query-aware Allocator (contextual bandit, cost-aware policy optimization) that decides per-frame visual resolution/budget before encoding, sitting on the accuracy-cost Pareto frontier and supporting up to 16x more frames at equal budget; this already delivers the per-frame-resolution half of Task-Aware Context Budgeting, minus RAG and the frame-vs-caption mix. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28610]
- **Q-Frame: Query-aware Frame Selection and Multi-Resolution Adaptation for Video-LLMs** (ICCV 2025): Training-free plug-and-play method (CLIP matching + Gumbel-Max) that jointly does query-aware frame selection AND per-frame multi-resolution scaling so the Video-LLM fits more useful content under a compute limit; evaluated on MLVU, LongVideoBench, Video-MME. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.22139]
- **Query-Conditioned Evidential Keyframe Sampling for MLLM-Based Long-Form Video Understanding** (arXiv 2026): Formulates keyframe selection as maximizing conditional mutual information between selected frames and the query (information-bottleneck grounding), decomposed into per-frame evidential scoring — i.e., the MI-style query-frame scoring objective DV-Pruner claims, albeit on single long videos. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01002]
- **Rethinking RAG in Long Videos: What to Retrieve and How to Use It? (CARVE + V-RAGBench)** (arXiv 2026): Operates directly on retrieved chunks in a video-RAG pipeline: chunk-adaptive reranking picks a per-chunk modality (frames vs text) and temporal granularity before generation, and V-RAGBench decouples retrieval from generation evaluation to expose retrieval errors; beats eight VideoRAG baselines. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13141]
- **BOLT: Boost Large Vision-Language Model Without Training for Long-form Video Understanding** (CVPR 2025): Training-free query-frame-similarity frame selection (inverse transform sampling) evaluated in a multi-source retrieval setting where relevant content sits inside a distractor-contaminated video pool — functionally query-aware pruning of distractors at inference time (Video-MME 53.8->56.1, MLVU 58.9->63.4). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.21483]
- **Decoupling Semantics and Logic: A Training-Free Coarse-to-Fine Pipeline for Video Retrieval-Augmented Generation** (ACL 2026 MAGMaR Workshop): Training-free video-RAG pipeline with an Adaptive-Iterative-Reasoning (A.I.R.) multimodal filtering agent that prunes semantically similar but logically irrelevant retrieved candidates so the generator sees only a distilled 'golden subset' — distractor pruning on retrieved evidence, the exact sandwich-middleware position DV-Pruner claims (workshop scope, competition eval). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07924]
- **VisionThink: Smart and Efficient Vision Language Model via Reinforcement Learning** (NeurIPS 2025): RL-trained VLM that starts from a downsampled image and autonomously decides per query whether to request the high-resolution version, deciding compression case-by-case instead of fixed pruning ratios; cuts visual tokens ~50% while keeping 102% of baseline accuracy across nine VQA benchmarks. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.13348]
- **Learning to Inference Adaptively for Multimodal Large Language Models (AdaLLaVA)** (ICCV 2025): Latency-aware learned scheduler that reconfigures MLLM operations at runtime given the input AND a hard latency budget, achieving varying accuracy-latency tradeoffs at inference time; composable with token selection. This is per-query, real-time, budget-constrained cost-quality adaptation. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10905]
- **Utility-Oriented Visual Evidence Selection for Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generation** (ACL 2026): Reformulates visual evidence selection for multimodal RAG through an information-theoretic lens: evidence utility is defined as the information gain induced on the model's output distribution (not semantic similarity), and a training-free framework with lightweight surrogate models ranks/selects visual evidence before generation, evaluated on MRAG-Bench and Visual-RAG. This both diagnoses the similarity-vs-utility disconnect and fixes it with a selection component. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13277]
- **Visual Haystacks: A Vision-Centric Needle-In-A-Haystack Benchmark (+ MIRAGE)** (ICLR 2025): Benchmark showing LMMs on multi-image QA are susceptible to visual distractors, fail cross-image reasoning, and exhibit positional bias; also proposes MIRAGE, a visual-RAG framework with a dynamic query-aware retriever that filters irrelevant images plus an 18x token compressor before the LMM. Diagnoses the exact failure modes the proposal motivates with AND ships a pruning-style middleware fix (relevance-based, not MI-saliency-based). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.13766]
- **Vgent: Graph-based Retrieval-Reasoning-Augmented Generation For Long Video Understanding** (NeurIPS 2025): Video RAG framework that retrieves clips from a structured video graph and then inserts an explicit intermediate structured-verification/reasoning step between retrieval and generation to filter retrieval noise and aggregate relevant cross-clip information before the LVLM answers; +3.0-5.4% over base models and +8.6% over prior video RAG on MLVU. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.14032]
- **Modality Relevance is not Modality Utility: Post-hoc Selective Modality Escalation for Cost-Aware Multimodal RAG** (arXiv 2026 (cs.IR)): Shows modality relevance is a weak predictor of whether visual evidence is needed for a correct answer; answers first from cheap text/table evidence, then a verifier plus a calibrated cost-aware router escalates to VLM visual processing only when justified, recovering full-VLM accuracy on MultiModalQA with far fewer visual calls. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05438]
- **When RAG Hurts: Diagnosing and Mitigating Attention Distraction in Retrieval-Augmented LVLMs (MAD-RAG)** (arXiv 2026 (cs.CV)): Diagnoses why retrieved context makes LVLMs fail questions they could answer without it (retrieved text suppresses visual attention and shifts it off question-relevant regions) and gives a training-free dual-question + attention-mixing fix that rectifies up to 74.7% of failure cases on OK-VQA/E-VQA/InfoSeek. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00344]
- **RULE: Reliable Multimodal RAG for Factuality in Medical Vision Language Models** (EMNLP 2024): Introduces a provably effective strategy for controlling factuality risk via calibrated selection of the NUMBER of retrieved contexts fed to a medical LVLM, plus preference fine-tuning to balance inherent vs retrieved knowledge (+47.4% factual accuracy). This is an existing risk-controlled, guarantee-bearing evidence-selection mechanism in multimodal RAG. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.05131]
- **Provence: Efficient and Robust Context Pruning for Retrieval-Augmented Generation** (ICLR 2025): Trains a lightweight DeBERTa model that, given the query and each retrieved passage, removes irrelevant sentences (binary sequence labeling) and unifies pruning with reranking in one model; plug-and-play middleware for any LLM/retriever (model itself is trained once, then frozen). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.16214]
- **RECOMP: Improving Retrieval-Augmented LMs with Compression and Selective Augmentation** (ICLR 2024): Inserts a query-conditioned compression step between retrieval and generation: an extractive compressor selects salient sentences and an abstractive (T5) compressor writes query-focused summaries, and can selectively augment (i.e., drop evidence entirely); improves QA with far fewer tokens. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.04408]
- **LongLLMLingua: Accelerating and Enhancing LLMs in Long Context Scenarios via Prompt Compression** (ACL 2024): Question-aware coarse-to-fine prompt compression middleware with budget-controlled dynamic compression ratios per document and reordering to fight position bias; boosts NQ accuracy up to 21.4% with ~4x fewer tokens, no generator training. [https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.91/]
- **The Power of Noise: Redefining Retrieval for RAG Systems** (SIGIR 2024): Systematic analysis of how distractor, random, and gold documents plus their position affect RAG answer accuracy, finding random noise can even help; directly prior art for the 'retrieval quality does not equal answer quality' framing (note the SIGIR 2026 follow-up 'The Powerless Noise' shows the effect is configuration-sensitive). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.14887]
- **Sufficient Context: A New Lens on Retrieval Augmented Generation Systems** (ICLR 2025): Google work that formalizes whether retrieved context is sufficient to answer, shows LLMs answer wrongly instead of abstaining even with(out) sufficient context, and uses the signal for abstention (+2-10pp selective accuracy); this is the text-RAG version of the journal's RQ6 retrieval-to-answer disconnect. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.06037]
- **CARES: Context-Aware Resolution Selector for VLMs** (ACL 2026): A 350M preprocessing module that predicts the minimal sufficient input resolution per image-query pair (discrete classifier, continuous interpolation at inference), cutting compute up to 80% at preserved accuracy; another occupied instance of query-adaptive resolution selection, though without explicit Pareto budgeting or RAG integration. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.19496]
- **MDP3: A Training-free Approach for List-wise Frame Selection in Video-LLMs** (ICCV 2025): Training-free, model-agnostic list-wise frame selection combining query relevance (query-conditioned Gaussian kernel), diversity (DPP), and sequentiality (Markov decision segmentation) with a (1-1/e) approximation guarantee — principled selection with theoretical guarantees, on single videos. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.02885]
- **Frame-Voyager: Learning to Query Frames for Video Large Language Models** (ICLR 2025): Learned (not training-free) query-aware selector trained to rank frame COMBINATIONS by the Video-LLM's own prediction loss, plugged into Video-LLMs as a module; strong on Video-MME, MLVU, NExT-QA, ActivityNet-QA. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.03226]
- **QuoTA: Query-oriented Token Assignment via CoT Query Decouple for Long Video Comprehension** (AAAI 2026): Training-free ante-hoc module that CoT-decouples the query and uses a lightweight scoring LVLM to assign the visual token BUDGET across frames by query relevance — query-aware budget allocation, overlapping the budgeting half of the proposal (though no resolution/caption mix or cost guarantee). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.08689]
- **VideoTree: Adaptive Tree-based Video Representation for LLM Reasoning on Long Videos** (CVPR 2025): Training-free query-adaptive hierarchical (coarse-to-fine) representation that expands tree depth only where query-relevant, extracting keyframes for LLM reasoning; SOTA training-free results on EgoSchema, NExT-QA, Video-MME long split. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.19209]
- **Too Many Frames, Not All Useful: Efficient Strategies for Long-Form Video QA (LVNet)** (EACL 2026 (earlier NeurIPS 2024 workshop)): Training-free modular Hierarchical Keyframe Selector that picks a minimal question-tailored frame subset before captioning/LLM answering; SOTA at comparable caption scale on EgoSchema, NExT-QA, IntentQA — its title is literally the proposal's motivating claim that selection beats accumulation. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09396]
- **FastV-RAG: Towards Fast and Fine-Grained Video QA with Retrieval-Augmented Generation** (arXiv 2026): Video-RAG framework that applies similarity-based filtering to retrieved knowledge (entity alignment) before the VLM and uses speculative decoding for ~2x inference speedup — evidence filtering plus latency optimization inside a video-RAG pipeline. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01513]
- **AdaReTaKe: Adaptive Redundancy Reduction to Perceive Longer for Video-language Understanding** (ACL 2025 Findings): Training-free, per-input allocation of compression ratios across video time chunks (via inter-frame similarity) and across KV-cache layers, with theoretical guarantees, expanding capacity from 256 to 2048 frames. Delivers adaptive budget allocation across video segments under a fixed context, i.e., very close to allocating budget across Top-K clips. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.12559]
- **HiRED: Attention-Guided Token Dropping for Efficient Inference of High-Resolution Vision-Language Models** (AAAI 2025): Under a fixed total token budget, uses ViT CLS attention to allocate a content-dependent budget to each partition of a high-resolution image, then keeps the most informative tokens per partition. Budget allocation across sub-images is a direct analog of allocating across multiple retrieved frames, though within a single image and without Pareto or cost modeling. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.10945]
- **Inference Optimal VLMs Need Fewer Visual Tokens and More Parameters** (arXiv 2024 (OpenReview)): Establishes compute-optimal scaling laws trading visual token count against LLM parameters under a fixed inference budget; finds error moves ~5x faster with LLM size than token count for reasoning (reversed for OCR), i.e., the optimal token budget is task-dependent. Offline scaling-law analysis, not per-query runtime allocation. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.03312]
- **VisPCO: Visual Token Pruning Configuration Optimization via Budget-Aware Pareto-Frontier Learning for Vision-Language Models** (arXiv 2026): Formulates visual token pruning configuration as a budget-aware Pareto optimization problem solved by gradient-based search (Augmented Lagrangian), finding layer-wise pruning patterns on the accuracy-efficiency frontier. Occupies the exact 'Pareto + token budget' framing, but the frontier is searched offline and applied statically, not optimized per query at inference. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15188]
- **Matryoshka Multimodal Models (M3)** (arXiv 2024): Trains nested coarse-to-fine visual token sets so the token count per image is explicitly controllable at inference, enabling flexible information-density vs efficiency tradeoffs per deployment (9 tokens suffice for COCO-style scenes, 144-576 for OCR/documents). Provides the control knob but not a per-query policy that chooses the operating point. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.17430]
- **SparseVLM: Visual Token Sparsification for Efficient Vision-Language Model Inference** (ICML 2025): Training-free, text-guided pruning: rates visual token significance using relevant text tokens via self-attention, with a rank-based layer-adaptive sparsification ratio and token recycling (61-67% FLOPs reduction at 93% accuracy). Query-conditioned relevance scoring of visual content overlaps both the budgeting knob and, at token granularity, the query-aware pruning idea. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.04417]
- **Adaptive-VoCo: Complexity-Aware Visual Token Compression for Vision-Language Models** (arXiv 2025): Lightweight rate predictor that allocates the visual token budget by input complexity, preserving fidelity for hard inputs and compressing aggressively on easy ones. Representative of a 2025-26 wave (also OccamToken, AdaptVision, ViCO, Q-Frame) of per-input budget-adaptive compression. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18496]
- **MRAG-Bench: Vision-Centric Evaluation for Retrieval-Augmented Multimodal Models** (ICLR 2025): 1,353 questions / 16,130 images benchmark measuring how well LVLMs utilize retrieved visual knowledge, including ground-truth (oracle) evidence conditions; finds GPT-4o gains only 5.82% from oracle evidence vs 33.16% for humans — a direct published analog of the journal's oracle-gap / utilization-disconnect finding, in image (not video) domain. Diagnostic only, no fix component. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.08182]
- **Evaluating Retrieval Quality in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (eRAG)** (SIGIR 2024): Shows query-document relevance labels correlate weakly with downstream RAG performance and proposes evaluating each retrieved document by the answer it induces from the LLM (downstream-grounded retrieval evaluation, Kendall's tau gains 0.168-0.494). Establishes the retrieval-metric vs answer-quality disconnect as known prior art in text RAG; its per-document downstream utility scoring is also conceptually adjacent to evidence pruning by utility. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.13781]
- **Long-Context LLMs Meet RAG: Overcoming Challenges for Long Inputs in RAG** (ICLR 2025): Google paper showing answer quality rises then FALLS as more passages are retrieved because hard negatives poison generation, and that stronger retrievers can make this worse; proposes fixes (training-free retrieval reordering, RAG-specific fine-tuning with intermediate reasoning). Diagnoses and fixes the recall-does-not-propagate phenomenon in text RAG. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05983]
- **Identifying and Mitigating Position Bias of Multi-image Vision-Language Models** (CVPR 2025): Demonstrates LVLM predictions swing strongly with the ordering of images in multi-image input (models favor end/beginning positions) and proposes SoFA, a training-free soft-attention interpolation fix improving multi-image benchmarks up to 10%. Directly preempts the 'position bias on multi-image evidence' part of the motivating diagnosis, with a model-side (not middleware) fix. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13792]
- **Lost at the End: Primacy Bias in Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Question Answering** (arXiv 2026): Controlled study of position bias specifically inside multimodal RAG evidence lists: gold-at-first beats gold-at-last by 16-26 points (a primacy reversal of lost-in-the-middle), and retrieval-side interventions (MMR, oracle reranking, reordering) fail to close the gap. Purely diagnostic — explicitly leaves the reader-side fix open, which supports the proposal's gap but also publishes its diagnosis first. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16494]
- **VLM Is a Strong Reranker: Advancing Multimodal RAG via Knowledge-enhanced Reranking and Noise-injected Training (RagVL)** (Findings of EMNLP 2025): Inserts an instruction-tuned VLM reranker with adaptive-threshold filtering between retrieval and generation to drop noisy/mismatched images (the multi-granularity noisy correspondence problem), plus noise-injected training of the generator; up to +40% retrieval accuracy and large QA gains. Image RAG, not video, and needs training, but it is exactly an evidence-filtering middleware stage. [https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.432/]
- **VideoRouter: Query-Adaptive Dual Routing for Efficient Long-Video Understanding** (arXiv 2026): Formulates post-routing compression as a budget-constrained optimization: dynamically computes the available visual-token budget from the model's max context length, prompt length, and reserved generation length, and that budget dictates frame dropping and pooling (effective resolution) strategies. Covers much of the context-window-driven budgeting idea, minus frames-vs-captions mix and any propagation guarantee. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05848]
- **AdaVideoRAG: Omni-Contextual Adaptive Retrieval-Augmented Efficient Long Video Understanding** (NeurIPS 2025): A lightweight intent classifier adaptively selects the retrieval scheme (from cheapest to most sophisticated) by query complexity, trading accuracy against redundant computation and latency in long-video RAG. Task/query-aware cost adaptation exists, but it adapts the retrieval path, not per-frame resolution or frame/caption token allocation. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.13589]
- **VideoRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Video Corpus** (Findings of ACL 2025): KAIST pipeline that dynamically retrieves relevant videos from a corpus and feeds both visual and textual content to an LVLM; already includes a video frame selection mechanism to extract the most informative frame subset and auxiliary text extraction when subtitles are absent. This is the proposal's own baseline and it already has a light selection stage. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.05874]
- **VideoRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Extreme Long-Context Videos** (KDD 2026): HKUDS system (LightRAG lineage) with graph-driven multimodal knowledge indexing and retrieval for hundreds-of-hours video corpora; establishes the scalable video-RAG system framing but has no saliency-based evidence pruner and no explicit context/GPU budget optimizer. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.01549]
- **Video-RAG: Visually-aligned Retrieval-Augmented Long Video Comprehension** (NeurIPS 2025): Training-free plug-and-play pipeline that extracts visually-aligned auxiliary texts (OCR, ASR, object detection) and injects them alongside video frames into any LVLM, averaging +8% on long-video benchmarks. Directly relevant to the frames-vs-text-captions mix dimension, but the mix is fixed by design rather than budget-optimized. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.13093]
- **NVIDIA AI Blueprint for Video Search and Summarization (VSS) with Context-Aware RAG** (NVIDIA (industrial reference architecture, 2025)): Production reference architecture for video analytics agents (summarization, visual Q&A, alerts on live CCTV-style streams) combining VLMs, LLMs, and a Context-Aware RAG module backed by both vector and graph databases. Not a research paper and no MI-based pruner or Pareto budgeter, but it substantially erodes the 'vector-DB + VLM video QA system' novelty framing, especially for surveillance use cases. [https://github.com/NVIDIA-AI-Blueprints/video-search-and-summarization]
- **MI-Pruner: Crossmodal Mutual Information-guided Token Pruner for Efficient MLLMs** (arXiv 2026 (cs.CV)): Prunes visual tokens by measuring mutual information between visual and textual features prior to their interaction, non-intrusively and without attention-map access, outperforming attention-based pruning; operates at token level inside MLLM inference rather than on retrieved evidence items, but claims exactly the crossmodal-MI pruning mechanism DV-Pruner proposes. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03072]
- **ForeSea: AI Forensic Search with Multi-modal Queries for Video Surveillance** (ECCV 2026): A VideoRAG system for long multi-camera surveillance footage: a tracking module filters irrelevant footage, multimodal embeddings index clips, and top-K retrieved clips feed a video LLM for QA with temporal grounding; claims to be the first VideoRAG built for this surveillance forensic setting and introduces the ForeSeaQA benchmark. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22872]
- **CFVBench: A Comprehensive Video Benchmark for Fine-grained Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generation (with Adaptive Visual Refinement)** (WWW 2026): 5,360-QA video-RAG benchmark that systematically evaluates 7 retrievers x 14 MLLMs, exposing that even GPT-5/Gemini miss transient fine-grained evidence after retrieval, and proposes AVR, a plug-and-play module that adaptively increases frame sampling density and invokes tools when needed. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.09266]
- **Retrieval Improvements Do Not Guarantee Better Answers: A Study of RAG for AI Policy QA** (arXiv 2026 (cs.CL)): Text-only study (ColBERT retriever + DPO-tuned generator on 947 AI-policy documents) showing that fine-tuning improves retrieval metrics without consistently improving end-to-end QA, and that stronger retrieval can yield more confident hallucinations — the same disconnect thesis as journal RQ6, in text RAG. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24580]
- **C-RAG: Certified Generation Risks for Retrieval-Augmented Language Models** (ICML 2024): First framework certifying an upper confidence bound on RAG generation risk via conformal risk analysis, with theory under distribution shift; proves conditions where RAG beats a lone LLM. Defines what a real 'guaranteed' RAG quality level requires — direct prior art against the '>=95% answer propagation guarantee' framing. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03181]
- **TRAQ: Trustworthy Retrieval Augmented Question Answering via Conformal Prediction** (NAACL 2024): End-to-end statistical correctness guarantee for RAG: conformal prediction sets over retriever and generator are aggregated so the correct answer is contained with a user-set probability (e.g. 95%), with Bayesian optimization shrinking set size. The literal published version of a 95%-level retrieval-to-answer guarantee, in text QA. [https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.210/]
- **Certifiably Robust RAG against Retrieval Corruption (RobustRAG)** (ICLR 2025): Isolate-then-aggregate defense giving certifiable lower bounds on RAG response quality even when a bounded number of retrieved passages are adversarially corrupted. Occupies the 'formal guarantee under noisy retrieved evidence' territory the proposal's guarantee claim gestures at, text-only. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.15556]
- **Corrective Retrieval Augmented Generation (CRAG)** (arXiv 2024): Lightweight retrieval evaluator scores retrieved documents and triggers corrective actions, with a decompose-then-recompose algorithm that splits documents into knowledge strips, filters irrelevant strips, and recomposes only key information before generation — a widely-cited evidence-pruning middleware in text RAG missing from the sweep. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.15884]
- **SURf: Teaching Large Vision-Language Models to Selectively Utilize Retrieved Information** (EMNLP 2024): Self-refinement fine-tuning that teaches LVLMs to use helpful retrieved multimodal references and resist irrelevant/misleading ones, built from positive vs negative reference pairs; improves robustness across 7 datasets. The multimodal analog of RAFT — generator-side distractor robustness for retrieved visual evidence. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.14083]
- **LongVU: Spatiotemporal Adaptive Compression for Long Video-Language Understanding** (ICML 2025): Text-guided cross-modal query selects which frame features to keep (after DINOv2 redundant-frame removal) and adaptively compresses spatial tokens by temporal dependency, fitting long videos into a fixed context. Query-conditioned frame/token budget reduction named in the sweep's prose but absent from the list. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.17434]
- **PruneVid: Visual Token Pruning for Efficient Video Large Language Models** (ACL 2025 Findings): Training-free method that merges spatiotemporal tokens and uses question-to-video ATTENTION weights from an intermediate LLM layer to keep only question-relevant visual tokens (>80% pruned at competitive accuracy). Already operationalizes 'attention x query-term relevance' scoring — the exact signal family DV-Pruner claims — at token level. [https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.1024/]
- **QUITO-X: A New Perspective on Context Compression from the Information Bottleneck Theory** (EMNLP 2025 Findings): Formulates query-guided context compression as information-bottleneck optimization and explicitly uses cross-attention scores as a proxy for mutual information between query and context. Publishes the precise 'attention-as-MI-with-query' scoring formalism DV-Pruner proposes, in the text domain. [https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.362/]
- **Zelda: Video Analytics using Vision-Language Models** (arXiv 2023 (VMware/Stanford tech report)): VLM-based top-K video query system that prompts with the natural-language query, auto-adds discriminator terms to remove low-quality/irrelevant frames, and prunes similar frames using relevance-and-diversity over VLM embeddings (7.5x faster at equal accuracy vs a SOTA analytics engine). Query-aware frame pruning inside a video analytics/DBMS stack, predating the proposal. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.03785]
- **Self-Enhancing Video Data Management System for Compositional Events with LLMs (VOCAL-UDF)** (SIGMOD 2025): Balazinska-group video DBMS that uses LLMs to synthesize missing UDFs (Python functions or distilled vision models) for compositional video event queries, with active learning to pick candidates. Anchor of the video-DBMS+LLM lane at the proposal's own target venue; the sweep mentioned this lane but listed no paper from it. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02243]
- **Twelve Labs Marengo + Pegasus video RAG stack (Weaviate/Pinecone/Chroma/Elasticsearch/Bedrock integrations)** (Industrial (Twelve Labs), 2024-2026): Production any-to-any video embedding model (Marengo) indexed in commodity vector DBs with a video-language generator (Pegasus) answering over retrieved segments — explicitly marketed as RAG over video that processes only query-relevant segments, available on Amazon Bedrock. Substantially erodes the 'vector-DB + VLM video QA' system novelty beyond the already-listed NVIDIA VSS. [https://www.twelvelabs.io/blog/twelve-labs-and-weaviate]
- **VideoDB: video database infrastructure for AI agents** (Industrial (videodb.io), 2024-2026): Commercial 'video database' backend unifying ingestion, scene/object/event indexing with timestamps, retrieval, and streaming for RAG and agents (LlamaIndex integration ships a VideoDB multimodal-RAG retriever), including live camera streams. Occupies the 'multimodal video DBMS for RAG' naming and framing of the proposal's title. [https://www.videodb.io/]
- **Learning to Filter Context for Retrieval-Augmented Generation (FILCO)** (arXiv 2023): Trains context-filtering models (lexical and information-theoretic signals, including a mutual-information-style utility measure) that filter retrieved passages at test time before the generator sees them; text-only and requires training the filter. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.08377]
- **RAFT: Adapting Language Model to Domain Specific RAG** (COLM 2024): Fine-tunes the generator with oracle-plus-distractor training mixtures so it learns to ignore irrelevant retrieved documents; attacks the same distractor-robustness problem but via model training, not middleware pruning. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.10131]
- **An Image is Worth 1/2 Tokens After Layer 2 (FastV)** (ECCV 2024 (Oral)): Foundational training-free method that prunes visual tokens with low attention scores after layer 2, giving ~45% FLOPs reduction without accuracy loss (up to 25% latency cut in video settings). Fixed pruning ratio, no per-query adaptivity, no cost-quality optimization. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.06764]
- **DivPrune: Diversity-based Visual Token Pruning for Large Multimodal Models** (CVPR 2025): Training-free, calibration-free pruning that selects tokens by solving a Max-Min Diversity Problem at a fixed budget; SOTA across 16 image and video datasets. Static budget, query-agnostic; same family as VisionZip (CVPR 2025), PyramidDrop (CVPR 2025), and LLaVA-PruMerge (ICCV 2025), all of which are likewise static-budget token reducers. [https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Alvar_DivPrune_Diversity-based_Visual_Token_Pruning_for_Large_Multimodal_Models_CVPR_2025_paper.html]
- **Text or Pixels? It Takes Half: On the Token Efficiency of Visual Text Inputs in Multimodal LLMs** (EMNLP 2025 Findings): Shows rendering long text as images nearly halves token cost without degrading performance, quantifying the cross-modality token exchange rate. It studies substitution of one modality for another, not joint budget allocation between image tokens and text captions, but it is the closest published work to the frames-vs-captions mix question. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.18279]
- **VideoHallucer: Evaluating Intrinsic and Extrinsic Hallucinations in Large Video-Language Models** (arXiv 2024): First comprehensive video-LLM hallucination benchmark using adversarial basic/hallucinated binary QA pairs; quantifies yes-bias via Yes Percentage Difference and False Positive Ratio across 11 models. Diagnoses the yes-bias failure mode the proposal cites, with no system fix; video-domain prior art for the bias measurement. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.16338]
- **Long Context Transfer from Language to Vision (LongVA / V-NIAH)** (arXiv 2024): Introduces V-NIAH, the visual needle-in-a-video-haystack test (needle frames inserted at varying positions in hours-long video), and LongVA, which extends context to ~2000 frames via language-side extrapolation. Establishes position-sensitivity diagnosis for video contexts; a capacity/model contribution, not an evidence-pruning or budgeting middleware. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.16852]
- **iRAG: Advancing RAG for Videos with an Incremental Approach** (CIKM 2024): NEC Labs system that replaces upfront video-to-text conversion with query-aware, on-demand incremental extraction from selected video portions (23-25x faster ingestion at comparable answer quality). Occupies the video-RAG systems-venue lane, but optimizes ingestion, not evidence pruning or generation-side token budgets. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.12309]
- **TrafficRAG: A Multimodal RAG Framework for Traffic Accident Liability Determination** (ICANN 2026): Uses a VLM to convert traffic-accident videos into structured scene descriptions that anchor hybrid BM25+dense retrieval of legal knowledge, then LLM reasoning produces liability reports; a domain collision (accident video + multimodal RAG) but with no evidence pruning or context budgeting. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01737]
- **syftr: Pareto-Optimal Generative AI** (AutoML 2025): Multi-objective Bayesian-optimization search over agentic and non-agentic RAG pipeline configurations that discovers Pareto-optimal accuracy-vs-cost flows (about 9x cheaper at near-equal accuracy); text-only and offline configuration search rather than per-query real-time budgeting, but owns the 'Pareto-optimal RAG' framing. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20266]
- **Seiden: Revisiting Query Processing in Video Database Systems** (VLDB 2023): Video DBMS query processing that samples frames and queries oracle models directly (multi-armed-bandit style exploration) instead of building proxy indexes — representative of the NoScope/BlazeIt query-aware frame-filtering lineage a SIGMOD reviewer will expect the related-work section to engage. [https://doi.org/10.14778/3598581.3598599]
- **SUTD-TrafficQA: A Question Answering Benchmark and an Efficient Network for Video Reasoning over Traffic Events** (CVPR 2021): 62,535 QA pairs over 10,080 in-the-wild traffic videos with causal/counterfactual reasoning, plus Eclipse, a computation-efficient dynamic reasoning network. The standard traffic-domain VideoQA benchmark the proposal's evaluation plan (VRU-Accident + AI Hub only) omits, inviting an external-validity objection. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.15538]
