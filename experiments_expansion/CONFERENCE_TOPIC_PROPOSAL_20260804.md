## 세 설계안 원문

- **설계 A**: 자연 상관 시공간 predicate 하의 filtered vector search 벤치마크+실험 연구 (VLDB EA&B / SIGMOD)
- **설계 B**: predicate별 부분 색인의 온라인 생성·파기 라이프사이클 관리자 (VLDB/SIGMOD research)

## 설계 A — 공백 검증 판정

The narrow premise survives verification: no public benchmark or experimental study evaluates filtered vector search under real, naturally correlated attribute-embedding workloads with a measured correlation spectrum — Big-ANN's YFCC tags are real but uncharacterized, VecBench and HCBGen inject correlation/hardness synthetically, ACORN and the PostgreSQL E&A study construct correlation regimes by pairing/sampling, RACORN-1 uses K-means labels, and the SIGMOD'26 in-depth study explicitly names vector-attribute interaction analysis as "still lacking." The strongest confirmation is MoReVec: the one paper that actually measured filter-embedding correlation (its GLS metric) on real attributes found rho ~ 0 on IMDb metadata, meaning the rho 0.62-0.895 regime your CCTV corpora occupy is empirically unvisited — but MoReVec also means you are NOT first to define or measure such a metric, so the claim must be "first benchmark whose real predicates span a measured natural-correlation spectrum," not "first to consider correlation." Three framings in Design A are already taken and need repositioning: "unified/systematic filtered-ANN evaluation" (PVLDB EA&B 2509.07789), "component-level mechanism analysis" (SIGMOD'26 2508.16263 covers entry-point/pruning/edge-filtering), and "correlation-controlled benchmark" (VecBench SIGMOD'26); contribution (3)/(4) survive only as "the 2025-26 generation (SIEVE/NaviX/CubeGraph/iRangeGraph) under measured natural correlation," which no existing study covers. HCBGen is a direct published counter-argument — it claims attribute-vector correlation is an unstable, strategy-inconsistent hardness proxy — so Design A must empirically show rho predicts real recall collapse (your 13x synthetic-vs-real misestimation result is exactly the right weapon) and should report alpha-hardness as a covariate to preempt that review. The window is short: five benchmark/study papers hit SIGMOD/VLDB/SIGIR in 2025-26 alone, MoReVec has already bolted filters onto ANN-Benchmarks, and arxiv-for-fanns's category attributes are latently correlated with embeddings, so any group could produce a "natural correlation" study within months. Venue appetite is proven (PVLDB EA&B and SIGMOD accepted four such papers this cycle), but EA&B expects public artifacts, so the AI Hub Korea-gating is a real reviewer risk: the MIRIS-plus-released-embeddings public track must be able to carry the headline collapse and correlation-spectrum results on its own, with the gated corpora as replication. Net: the gap is real but it is a 6-12-month, precisely-worded gap — "first benchmark with measured naturally correlated spatiotemporal predicates plus an end-to-end VQA track," and nothing broader.

## 설계 B — 공백 검증 판정

The narrow premise survives scrutiny: nothing found performs continuous ONLINE admission AND eviction of predicate-specific partial vector indexes under a joint memory+latency budget — SIEVE is verifiably offline (greedy submodular solve, explicit 'filter stability' assumption, workload shift handled by re-running construction, correlation term assumed constant c=1), extended Curator builds per-label indexes in batch with 'no eviction mechanism', Quake and CrackIVF are online but predicate-blind, and HONEYBEE's own §5.3 states that deciding when to repartition 'represents a substantial research challenge in its own right' and explicitly leaves 'adaptive repartitioning strategies' to future work — the strongest citable evidence the slot was open as of January 2026. The brutal caveat is that every ingredient of Design B already exists in isolation: budgeted sub-index selection (SIEVE, HONEYBEE's MINLP+greedy), online cost-modeled index adaptation (Quake), online build-cost amortization against cumulative query time for vector indexes (CrackIVF's α-constraint), and correlation formalization (ACORN; SIEVE's cor(w,f,h) term) — so the residual novelty is the online JOINT formulation plus correlation-as-control-signal, and a VLDB reviewer can compress it to 'SIEVE moved online with an eviction policy', which is precisely the framing HONEYBEE pre-announced as future work. The delta is therefore real but must be earned three ways: (a) empirically show that offline re-solve cadence fails under realistic drift with SIEVE-style rebuild as an explicit baseline; (b) give the admission/eviction rule formal teeth (ski-rental/competitive-ratio style online analysis — HONEYBEE cites online algorithms but does not do this); (c) prove the runtime correlation estimator beats a plain selectivity-times-frequency heuristic, because if it does not, the meta-layer collapses into a cache policy and the lab's rho=0.62-0.895 correlation asset carries no mechanistic weight. Claim wording must be exact: CatapultDB (Mar 2026) already does online workload-driven adaptation that preserves filtered search via shortcut-edge overlays, so 'first workload-adaptive filtered vector search' is false — the defensible claim is strictly 'first online lifecycle manager for predicate-specific partial indexes under joint memory+latency budgets'. Vendor docs confirm no shipped auto-materialization (Vespa static thresholds, Turbopuffer lazy per-namespace indexing plus cache tiering, Milvus schema-time partition key), so the 'generalizes vendor threshold heuristics' framing holds. Finally, concurrency risk is high: the filtered-ANN space produced at least four new papers in Feb-Aug 2026 alone, SIEVE's §7.7 workload-shift experiments telegraph an obvious online follow-up by its authors, and HONEYBEE's future-work sentence is an open invitation to that group too — treat this window as open but actively closing, which argues for fast execution and for anchoring the paper on the one asset competitors cannot replicate: the naturally correlated CCTV workloads and the 13x synthetic-vs-real difficulty gap.

## 설계 C — 공백 검증 판정

The narrow premise survives, but barely, and with a hostile new neighbor. Strictly nobody tunes vector-index PHYSICAL design (index type, partial-index materialization, ef/probe budgets) with a fitted downstream answer-utility objective: VDTuner (ICDE'24), DARTH (SIGMOD'26), and the July-2026 constraint-aware HNSW tuning paper all optimize recall/latency/size, my scan of 200+ eRAG citers found zero index-layer follow-ups, and arXiv queries for "physical design + vector database" and "downstream + ANN index" return nothing — so slot (2)+(3) of Design C is genuinely open. However, the flanking rhetoric is now occupied: "ANN Search: Recall What Matters" (arXiv, June 2026) explicitly argues Recall@k is the wrong ANN tuning objective, validates against LLM-graded RAG quality, and — worst for Design C — reports that downstream answer metrics remain STABLE under large recall drops, which is independent published corroboration of the lab's own +1.5pp p=0.448 null; IKS (ASPLOS'25) already made an end-to-end-accuracy-driven exact-vs-approximate physical-design argument; and syftr/METIS/AutoRAG-HP/CDS4RAG own accuracy-per-cost Pareto tuning of RAG configurations one layer up, so "answers-per-dollar" cannot be claimed as new framing, only as new at the index layer. Design C is therefore defensible only in its full conjunction: correlated-predicate collapse regime (recall −0.627, their unique measured asset) x index/materialization decision variables x properly powered causal propagation — no paper combines even two of these. The weak-signal risk is the real threat, and it is now double-edged: in the benign regimes everyone else studies, propagation is demonstrably weak, so a null in the collapse regime would make the utility-aware design rules degenerate to "cheapest index is always fine" and the contribution collapses to a (still publishable, EA&B-shaped) equivalence result — the +8.2pp oracle-vs-retrieved headroom (p=0.0006) proves retrieval can matter, but the halted manipulation gate proves delivering the treatment is hard, so the S4 protocol must be run only where evidence clips are predicate-specific (where a 0.627 recall collapse plausibly removes the actual evidence, not just near-duplicates). Recommendation: keep Design C but pre-register BOTH outcomes as headline results (divergence → utility-aware rules; equivalence via TOST → "recall collapse that never reaches answers, so recall-based tuning over-provisions" — itself a stronger, more contrarian EA&B claim that weaponizes their null instead of dying by it), and cite Recall-What-Matters as the unfiltered baseline their filtered regime extends. Shelf life is short: three of the closest papers are 2026 arXiv preprints, so the measurement claim has roughly two submission cycles before someone runs filtered-ANN-to-answers first.

### 설계 C 관련 논문
- [high] **ANN Search: Recall What Matters** (arXiv 2606.04522 (no venue yet as of Aug 2026) 2026): Argues the community should stop evaluating/tuning ANN on Recall@k and proposes 1/Ratio@k (inverse approximation ratio) instead; benchmarks SOTA ANN algorithms on efficiency, downstream classification, and RAG, explicitly measuring LLM-graded answer quality vs recall. Key finding: downstream indicators (label precision, BERTScore, LLM-graded quality) 'remain highly stable even when Recall@k drops significantly', and tuning to 1/Ratio hits operational quality at much lower compute. Occupies the 'recall is the wrong ANN tuning metric, judged downstream' rhetorical position — but its replacement is a geometric distance proxy, not a fitted answer-utility model, and it covers only unfiltered ANN with no physical-design (index type/materialization) decisions and no correlated-predicate regime.
- [medium] **Accelerating Retrieval-Augmented Generation (IKS)** (ASPLOS 2025 (arXiv 2412.15246) 2025): Profiles RAG pipelines and shows exact retrieval 'can send a smaller but more accurate list of documents to the generative model while maintaining the same end-to-end accuracy' than ANN variants, then builds IKS, a CXL near-memory accelerator for exact kNN (13.4-27.9x faster exact search, 1.7-26.3x lower end-to-end inference time). This is a physical-design decision (exact-search hardware vs approximate index) explicitly justified by end-to-end answer accuracy — the closest existing 'index approximation propagates to answers, so change the physical design' argument. But it is a coarse binary (exact vs ANN), hardware-centric, with no per-config utility model, no filtered search, and no tuning rules.
- [medium] **METIS: Fast Quality-Aware RAG Systems with Configuration Adaptation (a.k.a. RAGServe)** (arXiv 2412.10543 (v3 2025) 2024): First RAG serving system that jointly schedules queries and adapts per-query RAG configurations (number of retrieved chunks, synthesis method) to trade generation quality against response delay. Occupies per-query quality-vs-cost configuration adaptation, but the knobs are pipeline-level; it never touches the ANN index (no ef/nprobe, index type, or materialization) and quality estimation is heuristic, not a fitted utility model over index configs.
- [medium] **syftr: Pareto-Optimal Generative AI** (AutoML Conf 2025 (arXiv 2505.20266) 2025): Multi-objective Bayesian optimization over agentic/non-agentic RAG flows (vector stores, embedding models, splitters, retrievers, rerankers, LLMs) discovering Pareto-optimal accuracy-vs-cost/latency configurations. Directly occupies 'tune retrieval-stack configuration by downstream task accuracy per dollar'. GitHub README confirms the search space is LlamaIndex component-level; ANN index parameters (ef_search, M, nprobe, index type, partial-index materialization) are not decision variables. Any 'answers-per-dollar' headline must cite and differentiate from this.
- [medium] **PipeRAG: Fast Retrieval-Augmented Generation via Algorithm-System Co-design** (arXiv 2403.05676 (later KDD ADS) 2024): Decoder-side (RETRO-style) RAG co-design with a performance model that automatically balances retrieval quality against latency, adjusting database search parameters (search-space size) based on generation state and hardware. The closest prior work to 'tune ANN search budget with downstream awareness', but quality is measured by perplexity, the setting is periodic retrieval during decoding rather than QA answer accuracy, and index type/materialization are fixed.
- [medium] **CDS4RAG: Cyclic Dual-Sequential Hyperparameter Optimization for RAG** (arXiv 2605.08333 2026): Optimizes 'the full RAG hyperparameters' by cyclically alternating retriever-side and generator-side tuning under fine-grained evaluation budgets, evaluated on downstream query performance. Represents the maturing RAG-HPO line (with AutoRAG, AutoRAG-HP): downstream-metric-driven tuning of retriever hyperparameters exists, but published knobs are chunking/top-k/embedder-level; no evidence it tunes ANN index construction/search parameters or materialization.
- [medium] **Towards Robustness: A Critique of Current Vector Database Assessments** (arXiv 2507.00379 2025): Argues average recall is a misleading vector-DB metric because it hides tail queries that 'can lead to failure in downstream applications such as RAG'; proposes Robustness-δ@K (fraction of queries above a recall threshold) to guide index selection, and shows more robust indexes yield better application performance at equal average recall. Another occupant of 'recall as currently used is the wrong objective for downstream' — though the fix stays inside the recall family rather than modeling answer utility.

- [low] **AutoRAG-HP: Automatic Online Hyper-Parameter Tuning for Retrieval-Augmented Generation** (arXiv 2406.19251 (EMNLP Findings 2024) 2024): Online hierarchical multi-armed-bandit tuning of RAG hyperparameters (top-k, prompt compression ratio, embedding method) against downstream answer metrics. - [low] **DARTH: Declarative Recall Through Early Termination for Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search** (SIGMOD 2026 (arXiv 2505.19001) 2025): Per-query adaptive early termination on HNSW/IVF that meets user-declared recall targets with up to 14.6x/41.8x speedups, motivated by per-query recall variability. - [low] **VDTuner: Automated Performance Tuning for Vector Data Management Systems** (ICDE 2024 (arXiv 2404.10413) 2024): Multi-objective Bayesian-optimization auto-tuner for vector DBMS index and system parameters, balancing search speed against recall (venue confirmed via Semantic Scholar). - [low] **Exploiting Structural Properties for Efficient Constraint-Aware HNSW Hyperparameter Tuning** (arXiv 2607.04630 2026): Systems-perspective HNSW hyperparameter tuning exploiting monotonic/unimodal/separable structure in the config space under resource and tuning-time constraints; objectives are search quality (recall), latency, build time, and index size. - [low] **RAGO: Systematic Performance Optimization for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Serving** (ISCA 2025 (arXiv 2503.14649) 2025): Introduces RAGSchema abstraction and a serving optimizer that adapts scheduling/placement decisions to workload variability for performance (latency/throughput) targets. Answer quality is held fixed by the chosen RAG pipeline; it does not tune the ANN index or trade answer quality for cost.
- [low] **Stochastic RAG: End-to-End Retrieval-Augmented Generation through Expected Utility Maximization** (SIGIR 2024 (arXiv 2405.02816) 2024): Trains the RAG retriever end-to-end by maximizing expected downstream utility via differentiable Gumbel-top-k sampling. Establishes 'expected utility maximization' language in retrieval, but the decision variable is retriever model weights, not index physical design or search budgets.
- [low] **MRAG-Bench: Vision-Centric Evaluation for Retrieval-Augmented Multimodal Models** (ICLR 2025 (arXiv 2410.08182) 2024): Multimodal RAG benchmark for vision-centric scenarios comparing MLLMs with retrieved vs ground-truth visual knowledge (oracle-vs-retrieved gaps). The equivalence branch (TOST) at any defensible SESOI (2-3pp on 3-choice accuracy) needs on the order of 1,000+ effective paired questions IN STRATA WHERE THE TREATMENT ACTUALLY LANDS; the lab has 600+250 questions BEFORE conditioning on predicate-specific-evidence strata and before manipulation-gate attrition (prior delivery rate: 32 hits in 360 calls, ~9%). At that effective N the prior +1.5pp p=0.448 null is uninformative, not contrarian. Without an expanded, human-validated QA set and an a-priori power analysis covering BOTH branches, the paper has no claimable result.
- Design-space degeneracy at corpus scale. Verified: Weaviate's flatSearchCutoff defaults to 40,000 — filtered queries with allow-lists under 40k bypass HNSW and run brute force; Milvus has an analogous selectivity fallback. At 59k-142k vectors, the selective correlated filters that cause the 0.627 collapse are exactly the queries default production configs answer EXACTLY. If the utility-optimal physical design is 'exact prefilter everywhere' (likely at this scale), contribution (3) is vacuous and the paper reduces to 'brute force is fine at 100k vectors', which every vendor already ships. Must add a regime where approximation is genuinely forced: a scaled corpus (>=10M vectors with the measured correlation structure preserved) and/or hard latency SLOs, with exact prefilter as a first-class competitor arm.
- EA&B availability requirement vs AI Hub gating. Verified: PVLDB EA&B demands authors 'make available all experimental data and related software (there are no excuses)' plus a reproducibility commitment; AI Hub terms forbid providing data to unapproved third parties and require separate NIA agreements for foreign entities and for export abroad — plausibly covering derived embeddings — and the urban-road corpus provenance is unchecked. As designed (three-corpus EA&B), the target track's hard requirement is unmeetable for up to 2 of 3 corpora. Must restructure the artifact story (MIRIS-complete primary artifact, AI Hub as clearly-flagged non-artifact replication with regeneration scripts, written NIA clarification on embedding release, urban-road provenance resolved) or retarget the research track and accept its higher novelty bar.
- No committed path through a second manipulation-gate failure. Both headline branches are causal claims requiring the treatment (index approximation actually removing predicate-specific evidence clips) to be delivered; the prior attempt halted at this gate. Without a pilot that MEASURES evidence-removal rates per stratum and demonstrates delivery >= a pre-set threshold before the main powered run, the design has a single point of failure that produced zero publishable output last time.

### 주요 리스크
- Scoop within ~2 submission cycles (likelihood: medium-high). Three of the closest papers (RACORN-1, CubeGraph, HCBGen) are 2026 preprints from active groups, and the Recall-What-Matters authors need only add a filtered-predicate regime to own the whole territory. The measurement claim is perishable; every month of QA-set construction spends the window.
- Fitted U(config, stratum) fails to generalize (likelihood: high, if built naively). The lab's OWN prior result found learned answer-quality surrogates transfer NEGATIVELY across generators. A utility model fitted on three correlated CCTV corpora with one small VLM will be dismissed as a smoothed lookup table. Survivable only via a mechanism-structured model (config -> evidence recall -> P(correct | evidence)) validated leave-one-corpus-out and across >=2 VLMs.
- Equivalence-branch outcome reads as incremental (likelihood: medium). If TOST equivalence is the result, the paper becomes 'Recall-What-Matters replicated in the collapse regime' — publishable at EA&B but a likely reject at the research track; the divergence branch is the only outcome that yields novel design rules.
- Novelty-by-conjunction attack (likelihood: high at research track, medium at EA&B). SIEVE owns the materialization decision space, DARTH owns per-query budget adaptation, eRAG owns utility labels, IKS owns end-to-end-accuracy-driven physical design (coarse), syftr/METIS own answers-per-dollar one layer up. The paper is defensible only as measurement-first (EA&B framing) with the conjunction stated honestly.
- Cost-model credibility (likelihood: medium). 'Answers-per-dollar' priced from 2x RTX3090 with the lab's own prior note that latency was unisolated invites attack; cost deltas between index configs at 100k scale are noise-level. Needs an isolated measurement harness, device-seconds accounting, and sensitivity analysis over cost weights.
- Single-domain, single-language external validity (likelihood: medium). All three corpora are Korean CCTV traffic footage; reviewers will ask whether correlated-predicate collapse and its answer propagation appear in any other filtered-vector workload (e.g., YFCC tags, e-commerce facets); no second domain is currently planned.
- Compute/schedule slip (likelihood: medium). The grid (configs x questions x 2 VLMs x judges) matches the scale of the lab's completed 191x776x4 campaign, so GPU time fits, but human validation of new 3-choice questions and the gate pilot are serial human bottlenecks inside a two-cycle window.

### 예상 리뷰어 공격
- [Systems] At 142k vectors, your entire operating regime sits below Weaviate's default 40,000-object flat-search cutoff and Milvus's selectivity fallback — production systems already answer your collapse-inducing filtered queries exactly, by default. Why does vector-index physical design matter at all at your scale, and did your measured 0.627 collapse require disabling vendor fallbacks that real deployments leave on?
- [Systems] Your decision variables are SIEVE's (partial-index materialization under budget) and DARTH's (per-query search budgets) with the objective swapped from recall to eRAG-style answer utility. Swapping the y-variable in known machinery is not a systems contribution. What is technically new?
- [Methodology] Recall-What-Matters already shows downstream quality is stable under large recall drops, and your own pilot found +1.5pp at p=0.448. You now propose to run S4 only in strata hand-picked to maximize the effect. That demonstrates existence, not relevance: what fraction of a realistic CCTV query workload lives in your predicate-specific-evidence strata, and what is the workload-weighted answer delta?
- [Methodology] Show the power analysis for the TOST branch. With 3-choice outcomes, a 2-3pp SESOI, and your demonstrated 9% treatment-delivery rate, your effective paired N appears to be an order of magnitude short. A pre-registration that cannot reach power in either branch is theater.
- [Methodology] Your utility model is fitted and evaluated on three closely-related Korean CCTV corpora with one small open VLM, and your own prior work reports negative cross-generator transfer of learned quality surrogates. Why should any design rule extracted from U hold for a different corpus, VLM, or embedding model?
- [Benchmark/EA&B] EA&B rules say all experimental data and software must be made available, 'no excuses'. AI Hub forbids redistribution and foreign provision and one corpus has unresolved provenance. Concretely: how does an international reproducibility committee run your experiments?
- [Practitioner] Answers-per-dollar priced how? Two consumer 3090s with previously unisolated latency measurements do not yield a dollar figure I can act on, and at 100k-vector scale the cost differences between your index configs are within measurement noise.
- [Practitioner] Your +8.2pp oracle-vs-retrieved headroom conflates embedding/retriever error with index-approximation error. Decompose it: what part of that gap can ANY physical-design change recover, given a perfect index still misses evidence the embedding model never ranks highly?
- [All] Three of your closest competitors are 2026 arXiv preprints. If any of them publishes filtered-ANN-to-answers before your camera-ready, what of this paper survives? ACCEPTANCE CONDITIONS: we would accept a paper that (a) shows, on the SAME corpora, benign-regime stability (replicating Recall-What-Matters) side-by-side with a powered, gate-verified collapse-regime result in either pre-registered direction; (b) includes exact prefilter and a forced-approximation scale regime so the design rules are non-vacuous; (c) runs SIEVE-style same-memory-budget materialization under recall-objective vs utility-objective and shows the chosen configurations differ (or provably do not); (d) ships a MIRIS-complete public artifact; and (e) structures U as a mediation model validated leave-one-corpus-out across two VLMs.

### 실행 가능성
Engineering weight is genuinely light for this lab: every decision variable (index type, per-condition partial-index builds, ef/nprobe budgets, exact-prefilter arm) is reachable through Milvus/Weaviate/pgvector client APIs the lab already used for its tri-engine replication, and no C++/SIMD work is required anywhere in the design; index builds at 59k-142k vectors are minutes, and even the recommended >=10M scale-stress arm is a CPU/RAM job of hours on the lab server, not a GPU job. The binding compute is VLM inference for the answer layer: dozens of configs x ~1-2k questions x 2 VLMs x judging is the same order as the lab's already-completed 191-config x 776-question x 4-judge campaign on the same 2x RTX3090 + server setup, so 2-4 months of compute is realistic, especially if answer-layer evaluation is restricted to queries whose retrieved sets actually differ between configs. The true schedule constraints are human and serial: expanding and human-validating the 3-choice QA set to reach TOST power, running the manipulation-gate pilot with measured evidence-removal rates, and obtaining written AI Hub clarification on embedding release. Six to nine months to a PVLDB monthly deadline is achievable, but the two-cycle scoop window leaves no slack for a second gate failure, so the pilot must be the first thing executed.

### 수정 처방
- Run the manipulation-gate pilot FIRST (weeks, not months): select strata where the ground-truth evidence clip is predicate-specific (high rho subset), measure per-stratum evidence-removal rate under each index config, and pre-commit a go/no-go threshold; only strata passing the gate enter the powered study. This converts the prior halt from a failure mode into a screening step.
- Do the power analysis before anything else and size the QA set to it: pre-register SESOI (e.g., 3pp), compute McNemar and TOST power at the measured delivery rate, and expand to the required N with newly generated, human-validated 3-choice questions concentrated on gate-passing strata; use the planned IPW stratified sampling to buy back efficiency.
- Replace the flat 'fitted U(config, stratum)' with a two-stage mediation model: config -> per-stratum evidence recall -> P(correct | evidence present/absent). This makes recall an explicit mediator (directly quantifying 'how much recall loss reaches answers'), is estimable at modest N, transfers better than a direct regression, and preempts the 'smoothed lookup table' attack. Validate leave-one-corpus-out and on a second VLM.
- Add exact prefilter (brute force) as a first-class design arm and report where it wins — embrace, don't hide, the Weaviate flatSearchCutoff=40k / Milvus fallback reality; then add a forced-approximation regime (scale the corpus to >=10M vectors preserving the measured correlation structure, and/or impose latency SLOs) so the design rules have a non-degenerate domain. State explicitly which regime real CCTV deployments occupy.
- Make the cleanest possible divergence demonstration: same memory budget, SIEVE-style partial-index selection solved twice — once with recall objective, once with answer-utility objective — and show the selected materializations and resulting answers-per-dollar differ (or certify equivalence via TOST). This is the single experiment that proves contribution (3) is real.
- Position Recall-What-Matters (arXiv 2606.04522, verified) as the unfiltered-regime baseline and REPLICATE its stability finding on your own corpora in the benign regime, side-by-side with the collapse regime; the paper's identity becomes 'regime map: where recall loss reaches answers and where it provably does not', which weaponizes either pre-registered outcome.
- Fix the artifact story before submission: MIRIS-complete public pipeline (embeddings, indexes, QA, code) as the primary reproducibility artifact; AI Hub corpus as clearly-flagged replication with regeneration scripts and config hashes for credentialed users; request written NIA/AI Hub clarification on derived-embedding release; resolve urban-road provenance now or drop it. If AI Hub clarification fails, state the limitation explicitly rather than letting the reproducibility committee discover it.
- Decompose the +8.2pp oracle headroom into retriever-error vs index-approximation-error components (oracle vs exact-index-retrieved vs approximate-index-retrieved three-way comparison) so the recoverable-by-physical-design fraction is explicit.
- Build the cost model defensively: isolated latency harness (the lab already learned this lesson), device-seconds and $-per-1k-queries under published cloud GPU pricing as sensitivity bands, and report answers-per-dollar as rankings robust across cost-weight choices rather than point dollar figures.
- Target a specific PVLDB monthly deadline within two cycles with EA&B framing ('[Experiment, Analysis & Benchmark]' title tag, 12pp), pre-register on OSF with both headline branches, and cite RACORN-1/CubeGraph/HCBGen in the pre-registration to timestamp priority on the filtered-regime-to-answers question.

Best-grounded: (1) scale and overload — 2.05M public CCTV cameras (2025, official e-나라지표) monitored through 216-217 control centers at 481 cameras per operator versus the 50-camera MOIS standard, which the ministry itself calls 'close to impossible', making automated retrieval structurally inevitable; (2) AI video SEARCH is already operational, not hypothetical — Seoul's AI 기반 CCTV 고속 검색 시스템 does attribute-conditioned retrieval (appearance + direction, i.e., exactly a filtered/predicated search) over 123,000 cameras citywide since 2026, used in 1,600+ missing-person/incident cases, and MOIS mandates nationwide AI 관제 by 2027; (3) the certification gap is factually checkable — KISA's 지능형 CCTV certification (the only national one, backed by TTA TTAK.KO-10.0904) certifies only per-event detection at a 90% threshold via GT-file comparison and tests nothing about retrieval recall, so 'no current certification can detect silent recall loss' is literally true for Korea; (4) the embedding-release design is legally well-motivated — PIPA Art. 25 purpose-limits CCTV footage and PIPC practice default-prohibits raw-video release for AI work (mosaic/pseudonymization required, with raw use possible only via sandbox exemptions confined to data trust zones), so an embedding-level benchmark is the only distribution path that avoids case-by-case exemption. Shakier elements: dementia missing-person counts vary slightly across outlets (15,836 vs 15,502 for 2024 — cite as '약 1만5천여 건' or fetch the 경찰청 table directly), the 50-camera standard and Joongang's 77.2%/man-weeks figures are consistent across outlets but were not verified against primary documents, and note the 62.7% recall-loss figure is the lab's own result with no external Korean anchor — do not imply otherwise. Single strongest verified fact for the paper: Seoul completed an AI-based CCTV fast-search system across all 25 districts in 2026 that retrieves persons by appearance and direction over 123,000 cameras (100 hours of footage in under 10 minutes, 1,600+ real missing-person/incident uses) while the only national certification regime (KISA, 90% event-detection threshold) tests no retrieval accuracy at all — the exact deployment-versus-evaluation mismatch the benchmark targets.

# [추가 2026-08-04c] 범용성·연구 정체성 재설계 (사용자 2차 지적 반영)

사용자 지적: (1) 데이터셋 도메인은 멀티모달 전반을 수용하는 범용성 필요, (2) 새 알고리즘도 SOTA 벤치마크도 아니면 연구 논문으로서 역할이 불명 — 경쟁력 재소명 요구.

## 재설계 골자
- 연구 대상을 "CCTV"에서 **"자연 상관(natural predicate-embedding correlation)이라는 축 그 자체"**로 승격. CCTV는 스코프가 아니라 고상관 극단 영역의 앵커 + 사회적 동기.
- 벤치마크를 고정 데이터셋이 아니라 **플러그인 하니스**로 정의: 입력 = (임베딩, 메타데이터 테이블, 선택적 GT) → 출력 = 상관 프로파일(ρ/GLS/α-hardness), 층화 워크로드, 위험 리포트, 엔진 평가. 임의의 멀티모달 코퍼스 수용.
- 레퍼런스 인스턴스로 3개 모달리티 스펙트럼 커버: 텍스트(arxiv-for-fanns 2.7M, 실측 속성), 이미지(YFCC-10M, 실측 태그), 비디오(CCTV 3코퍼스, ρ 0.62-0.895 극단) + 저상관 앵커(MoReVec IMDb ρ≈0).
- 기여 3층 스택: L1 현상+경험 법칙(상관 스펙트럼 위치가 붕괴를 예측 — 교차 도메인 검증, HCBGen "불안정 프록시" 주장의 자연상관 반증), L2 측정 방법론+하니스, L3 기술적 산출물 = **상관 인지 신뢰성 어드바이저**(배포 전 (코퍼스, 워크로드, 인덱스 구성)→재현율 위험 예측 + 완화 결정 규칙; leave-one-domain-out 검증). SIEVE 비용모델의 상관항 c=1 가정이 "이 능력이 부재하다"는 인용 가능한 증거.
- 경쟁력 근거: 같은 장르(측정 연구)가 이 서브필드에서만 18개월간 6편 게재/예정(PVLDB EA&B 2025 통합연구, SIGMOD 2026 in-depth, VecBench, HCBGen, arxiv-for-fanns SIGIR 2026, PostgreSQL E&A). 전부 합성/무상관 — 자연 상관 스펙트럼+교차 도메인 법칙+어드바이저는 미점유.
